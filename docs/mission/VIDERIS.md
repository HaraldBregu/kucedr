# Videris (Blackdot Solutions)

Research note for the [company-brief direction](RESEARCH.md). Read 2026-11-25 from the sources at the end.
"Not stated" means the pages read did not say.

## At a glance

- **Company:** Blackdot Solutions Limited, UK (address not stated on the pages read). CEO Stuart
  Clarke. Founding year not stated; "We began developing Videris in 2014".
- **Product:** Videris, an investigations platform that "brings open source, commercial and internal
  data into one secure platform". It includes Videris Investigate, the Videris Investigative
  Assistant (VIA) and Videris Automate.
- **Customers:** tax authorities, FIUs, defence, national security and law enforcement; banks,
  insurers and regulated firms; corporate risk teams; corporate intelligence and law firms. Named
  testimonials include Danske Bank, CERTA and the Jersey FIU.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001:2022, SOC 2 and Cyber Essentials Plus (product page).

## Mission

> "Our mission is to help investigators use OSINT for good by equipping them with the software they
> need" (about page)

## What they sell

- **Videris Investigate:** federated search across open, commercial and internal sources; secure,
  anonymous browsing "with a full audit trail and evidence capture"; entity extraction,
  translation, network visualisation, enrichment and monitoring.
- **VIA:** an AI assistant for analysing cases, described as "safe, explainable, configurable".
- **Videris Automate:** announced September 2025 (Help Net Security). Playbooks automate collection,
  "network and risk analysis" and reporting with LLMs and agentic AI, with "human investigators"
  able to "remain in the loop".
- **Extension Builder:** connects third-party and internal sources by API or MCP server. Over 50 data
  partners, including Moody's, LSEG, LexisNexis and OpenCorporates.
- **Professional services:** Blackdot Accelerate consultancy, integrations and training.

## Prices

No prices on blackdotsolutions.com, which offers a demo. Blackdot's G-Cloud 15 pricing document
(V1.1, August 2026), in pounds, named-user licences "payable annually in advance"; VAT not stated:

- **Platform fee:** £30,000 a year, including 4 named users with Videris Investigate, VIA and the
  built-in data sources.
- **Extra named users:** £7,000 a year each for licences 5 to 50, falling to £6,500 (51 to 100),
  £6,000 (101 to 150), £5,500 (151 to 250) and £5,250 (251 and above).
- **Videris Automate and monitoring:** Automate Credit Bundle, £2,000 per 1,200 credits.
- **Add-ons:** advanced social media extension £2,500 per user a year; premium data source credit
  bundle £10,000 each.
- **Services:** Accelerate Essentials £25,000 a year; Accelerate Elite £40,000 a year; integration
  £1,250 per developer per day; extra training £1,200 per 2-hour session.
- **Terms:** 5% discount for contracts of 2 years or more. Trials "for up to 4 weeks for internal
  evaluation purposes" (G-Cloud listing).

The reseller Internet Investigation Solutions Ltd lists Videris on G-Cloud 15 through its Long Arm
platform; its pricing document shows the same figures.

## Evidence and AI

- "Videris logs every action taken throughout an investigation, with full sourcing across all data
  types." Reports are "Automated, sourced reports".
- G-Cloud listing: "Automate evidential source capture and audit capability"; audit information is
  kept "At least 12 months"; exports in PDF, Word and CSV, with JSON "in 2026".
- Hashes and screenshots: not stated.
- AI: GenAI is used "to automate report creation" (FAQ), and the G-Cloud listing says Automate
  produces "human-reviewable reports". Models not named; "customer data is never used to train AI".
- Hosting: "a multi-tenanted SaaS web application hosted on AWS servers in the UK and EU" (FAQ); the
  G-Cloud listing gives the United Kingdom. Long Arm customers can reach it through the Long Arm
  Desktop.
- Vendor claims: "5x" faster time to insight and "80%" less manual work.

## Compared with Kucedr's direction

- Videris is the enterprise version of the brief workflow: collection with an audit trail, an AI
  first draft, human review, sourced reports and Word export, plus licensed corporate and risk data
  that Kucedr does not have.
- Price signal: the smallest published purchase is £30,000 a year for four users, before Automate
  credits, against the research's €250 assisted pilot. Its buyers are banks, governments and
  corporate risk teams.
- What Kucedr can own: a single analyst's machine and model, the firm's own report template, and a
  reviewer reopening the preserved page. Videris is sold as SaaS hosted in the UK and EU.
