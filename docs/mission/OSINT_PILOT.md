# OSINT Pilot

Research note for the [product direction](PRODUCT.md). Read 2026-11-25 from the sources at the end.
"Not stated" means the pages read did not say.

## At a glance

- **Company:** built by Pentester.com. The terms name Pentester, LLC, a Florida company; the about
  page says Pentester, Inc. Based in Boca Raton, Florida, USA. Pentester was established in 2024.
- **Status:** "in closed beta" (about page).
- **Customers:** law enforcement, fraud units, private investigators and security teams. "It is
  not a consumer people-search product."

## Mission

> "Run comprehensive OSINT investigations in minutes, not days."

## What they sell

- Investigations run by six AI agents over "30+" sources (breach data, social platforms, public
  records and the open web), started from an email, a phone number or a name.
- Results come as sourced reports with confidence scores, exported as Markdown or PDF.
- Matched photo search, credited one to one with investigations.
- Annual licences for agencies and government units, paid by invoice.

## Prices

In US dollars. Credits are bought once and never expire; one credit is one investigation, and
failed runs are refunded:

- 10 credits: $200
- 50 credits: $950
- 100 credits: $1,800
- 250 credits: $4,250
- 500 credits: $8,000

Annual agency licences:

- **Standard:** $15,000 for 1,000 investigations.
- **Professional:** $27,500 for 2,000.
- **Plus:** $40,000 for 3,000.
- **Enterprise:** $55,000 for 4,000.
- **Government Enterprise:** from $70,000 for 5,000 or more.

Free tier or trial: not stated.

## Vetting and data

- "Every account is reviewed before activation", each search is screened by AI, and every search
  needs a lawful purpose.
- Logs record the user, timestamp and IP address.
- Investigation data goes to third-party language-model providers. The privacy policy says OSINT
  Pilot does not train models on it, but also that a provider without an opt-out "may train on the
  data we send it".
- Hashes are mentioned once in the terms, without detail.

## Compared with Kucedr's direction

- OSINT Pilot investigates people from an email, phone number or name: the people-search use the
  product direction keeps out of Kucedr's bundled tools. Its account vetting shows how a vendor in
  that space protects itself.
- It charges per result, about $13 to $20 an investigation, not per seat.
- Everything runs in its cloud with its own sources; nothing runs on the investigator's machine.
