# Paying markets worldwide

Status: proposal, written 2026-11-26. Nothing here is decided or built. It answers the owner's
question: which important markets would pay for Kucedr, and in which region first. Read for it:
[MISSION.md](MISSION.md), [DIRECTIONS.md](DIRECTIONS.md), [FRONTIER.md](FRONTIER.md), and a dossier
of eight discovery angles, 26 regional and global research tracks (each checked by a second pass)
and three judges' rankings of ten shortlisted directions. "Secondary" marks a figure from press, an
aggregator, a market-research firm or a search snippet. Verifiers' corrections are carried; refuted
claims are dropped. The shared web-search budget ran out early in most tracks, so many regions were
studied only through pages fetched directly. Read "none found" as "not established", not as proof of
absence. Gaps are [at the end](#not-verified).

Rates: EUR 1 = USD 1.0935 and USD 1 = BRL 5.90 (ECB, 25 Nov 2026); USD 1 = INR 92.53 (X-Rates,
26 Nov 2026). GBP, CAD, JPY, KRW and CNY conversions use assumed rates (GBP 1 ≈ USD 1.27, USD 1 ≈
CAD 1.39, JPY 150, KRW 1,400, CNY 7.1) and are approximate.

## Summary

- **No direction has verified evidence that anyone pays for Kucedr's own offer.** Every money
  signal found is a competitor's price, a funding round or a government award. This changes nothing
  in [DIRECTIONS.md](DIRECTIONS.md)'s finding that privacy alone does not sell: no region showed a
  premium for processing on the device.
- **Of the regions studied, the paying markets that matter are the USA and English-speaking Europe**
  (UK, then the EU with Italy as home). China, India, Japan, South Korea and Latin America show no
  paying buyer Kucedr can reach now. They are places for free-core users and contributors, not first
  places to sell. Singapore, the UAE, Switzerland and Australia were not researched.
- **First direction: an investigation and due-diligence desk, built once and sold to two buyers.**
  D01 (small investigation firms) has the most primary prices of any direction. The self-serve ones
  a local tool competes with are Hunchly EUR 149 a year and Forensic OSINT USD 197 to 1,097 a year.
  Silo (remote isolation, USD 1,450 to 3,450 per user), Maltego (enterprise, EUR 3,000 to 7,500;
  Entry for vetted users only) and Videris (public sector, GBP 30,000 for four users on G-Cloud 15)
  show the top of the market, not Kucedr's price band. D02 (DD boutiques and AML consultants) needs
  almost the same build.
- **First region for D01 and D02: the UK and the USA in English, Italy in Italian as the home
  channel.** The money evidence is strongest in the USA. Italy has no D01 money evidence and is
  chosen only because access costs nothing. Both English markets need signed builds first.
- **Side test: the Italian studio desk (D05).** It moves from first in DIRECTIONS.md to a cheap
  ten-studio test. D05 has the only Italian primary prices for small practices; it is demoted on
  Kucedr's edge, not on lack of spending. Lexroom starts at EUR 99 a month, and Legora and Harvey
  are far larger than DIRECTIONS.md assumed.
- **Five gates apply to every paid direction in every region:** a legal entity so builds can be
  signed; encryption on by default; local embeddings; [FRONTIER.md](FRONTIER.md)'s real-Tor and
  real-proxy test passed; and one recorded case run with a local model using tools and the browser.
  Until then, data does not stay on the machine and the route claim is not proven.
- **Changes to [FRONTIER.md](FRONTIER.md): none in direction, one in weight.** Hostile-link triage
  (D03) stays a mode of the investigation desk. Much recent federal OSINT money goes to immigration
  and border location and identity tracking (Babel Street, USD 10.3M for 25 ICE licences; Penlink
  at ICE; DEA's ShadowDragon award, USD 12.56M, is larger). The mission rules that work out.
- **Before writing code, pre-sell.** One hour with the Italian lawyer, then about 20 buyer calls in
  1-19 December or 7 January-4 February 2027, a stated price and a pass mark
  ([first test](#first-test)).

**Dropped, and why.** Anti-detect browsers serve account farming. Clinicians: a cloud BAA meets the
rule and the Voice rule blocks transcription. Pharmacovigilance, e-discovery, auditors, US federal
AI, EU sovereign tenders, bank on-prem AI: certification and procurement beyond one developer.
Equity research: licensed data. MSPs: PSA and RMM bundle AI free. Monitoring, geo QA, extraction,
translation, newsroom and narrative desks: no privacy reason or no fit. Whistleblowing: incumbents
from EUR 79 a month, needs a server. Legal aid, fact-checkers, pentest and vulnerability triage:
grant channels or already rejected in [FRONTIER.md](FRONTIER.md).

**What this means for Kucedr.** Buyers pay for capture that holds up, managed attribution,
licensed data and certifications. The only discovered market where buyers pay self-serve, in
Kucedr's price band, is online investigation, for parts Kucedr has in early form: per-tab routes
through the user's own proxy or Tor, local storage, and screenshot or PDF capture. Kucedr does not
yet have hashed, timestamped capture, managed egress regions or proxy logins, and those are exactly
what Hunchly and Silo charge for.

## The recommendation in detail

**Region and language.** The UK and the USA first, in English; Italy in Italian as the home
channel. The money evidence is strongest in the USA. Italy has no D01 money evidence and is chosen
only because access costs nothing. Both English markets need signed Windows builds before the first
paid seat. Canada next. Nowhere else for paid work.

**Buyers.** In order: small UK investigation firms (ABI directory), small US firms doing insurer SIU
work, and Italian agenzie investigative; UK, US and Italian DD boutiques; outsourced AML and KYC
consultants serving fintech, payment and crypto firms. Solo PIs only as a secondary channel. Insurer
SIUs are a second step and may outsource.

**Product rules.** Carry [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md) rules 1 to 5 as written. Sell: per-tab routes
(direct, private, Tor, or the customer's own authenticated proxies), no fallback; a capture record
(URL, UTC time, route, SHA-256); an encrypted case container; an agent-drafted chronology or memo
where every claim links to a capture; a cited PDF export with a documented integrity method. No people-search, "anonymity" or "hacking" framing. Screening data only
through the customer's own key. The human concludes.

**What to build from the roadmap**, only after the first test passes, except the gates:

prepay. If D01 and D02 both fail, keep capture in the free core and stop chasing investigation
