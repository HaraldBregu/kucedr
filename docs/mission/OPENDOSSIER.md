# OpenDossier

Research note for the [company-brief direction](RESEARCH.md). Read 2026-11-25 from the sources at the end.
"Not stated" means the pages read did not say.

## At a glance

- **Company:** OpenDossier, "A Magellan Global brand" of Magellan Global Group, with offices in
  Singapore (group headquarters), Tokyo and Taipei. Founding year not stated.
- **People:** the founder is not named; the about page describes them as "a USPTO-registered patent
  practitioner and an AI researcher".
- **Product:** a company reviews and due-diligence platform. Verified client and employee reviews
  sit beside official records, with reports, a diligence workspace, monitoring and an API and MCP
  server.
- **Coverage:** United States and Singapore are live; Hong Kong, Taiwan and Japan are next; Canada,
  Australia, New Zealand, the UK and Malta are on the roadmap. Companies only, "never consumer
  reports on individuals".
- **Customers:** not named; "companies and researchers" pay for reports, briefs, monitoring and
  profile management.

## Mission

> "We make it possible to understand a company with traceable evidence, and to be understood the
> same way." (about page, not labelled as a mission)

## What they sell

- **For investigators:** Company Intelligence, Diligence Workspace and Company Monitor.
- **Reports:** a free Company Snapshot, the Company Report, the analyst-reviewed Diligence Report
  and the Analyst Diligence Brief, "a scoped investigation for a decision".
- **For companies being checked:** free profile claiming, Profile Pro, Trust Pro, Trust Workspace,
  AI Assurance Dossier, certification readiness and AI Visibility.
- **For systems and agents:** a REST API, webhooks and an MCP server.

## Prices

From the pricing and product pages, in US dollars; VAT or tax not stated.

- **Company Snapshot:** free, instant.
- **Company Report:** US$49, generated on demand and refreshed within 30 days; ten for US$390.
- **Diligence Report:** from US$490, "Five business days, analyst-reviewed", with up to three client
  reference calls and 30 days of monitoring.
- **Analyst Diligence Brief:** from US$1,000 per subject, three to twelve business days by depth,
  workspace included. Add-ons: client interviews +US$600, AI supplier question set +US$400,

- **Researcher plans:** Reader US$19 a month; Pro Researcher US$149 per seat per month (five Company

- **Company Intelligence:** US$99 per seat per month (product page).
- **Monitoring and data:** Company Monitor from US$600 a month for up to 25 companies (Monitor Team
  US$600 to 1,500 a month); API and MCP from US$1,500 a month; data licensing quoted.
- **Company plans:** Profile Pro US$199 a month; Trust Pro US$599 a month; Trust Workspace from
  US$200 per company per month; AI Visibility from US$150 a month; AI Assurance Dossier US$5,000 to

## Evidence and AI

- Each important claim carries a chain: "Source → original location → document date → retrieval
  date → entity match → verification status → contradictions". Statuses are Verified, Partial,
  Company-stated, Licensed / supplied, Open question and Contradiction.
- Source limits are written on the claim, and an absent record is reported "with its search date and
  scope", not as a finding of no risk.
- "An analyst reviews every brief before it is released"; the reviewer is named and dated.
- Exports: the Company Report is "PDF plus machine-readable JSON and Markdown copies"; the Diligence
  Workspace gives a "PDF report with the evidence chain as an appendix", a "CSV of claims" and a
  client share link. Word is not mentioned.
- Saved copies of sources, snapshots and hashes: not stated.
- AI: how AI produces findings is not stated. Profiles and reports are published for AI assistants
  (llms-full.txt, Markdown, API and MCP).
- Hosting: not stated; "your notes and documents stay private to your workspace".

## Compared with Kucedr's direction

- OpenDossier sells the outcome the research's buyer produces: an analyst-reviewed brief with a
  per-claim evidence chain, from US$1,000 per subject. It competes with the boutique firm as much
  as with Kucedr.
- Its chain fields (document date, retrieval date, entity match, verification status,
  contradictions) match the qualification the research says must survive editing and export.
- It does not cover the research's demo cases yet: the UK is on the roadmap and Italy is not listed.
  Whether a reviewer can reopen the page as it was retrieved is not stated.
- Price signal: US$49 for a standard company report and US$99 to US$149 per seat a month for
  research access, alongside NC Data's €22.50 report as the low anchor.
