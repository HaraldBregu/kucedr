# AegisStack

Research note for the [product direction](PRODUCT.md). Read 2026-11-25 from the sources at the end.
"Not stated" means the pages read did not say.

## At a glance

- **Company:** not stated. The site names no legal entity, location, founding year or team.
- **Product:** an AI-powered OSINT platform for cybersecurity investigations.
- **Customers:** cyber threat intelligence, law enforcement, corporate security, due diligence,
  fraud investigation and geopolitical intelligence. It claims "security teams at Fortune 500
  companies", unnamed.

## Mission

Not stated. The closest is the product line: "AegisStack accelerates cybersecurity investigations
by deploying AI agents with access to 150+ OSINT tools."

## What they sell

- AI agents that run across 150+ OSINT tools, with graph, timeline and map views and network
  mapping.
- Training, data or consulting: not stated.

## Prices

- Not published; the pricing address on its site returns "not found".
- 14-day free trial, no credit card required.

## Evidence and data

- "AI agents never train on your investigation data"; "military-grade encryption" at rest and in
  transit.
- Chain of custody, hashing and exports: not stated, although the law-enforcement copy promises
  "audit-friendly, warrant-ready intelligence".
- The site says "Data never leaves your secure environment" without explaining how that fits with
  searches across external tools.

## Not to be confused with

- **AEGIS (aegis-osint.com):** a separate OSINT platform with public prices: Community free, Pro
  $29 per user a month with a 14-day trial, Enterprise $99 per user a month.

## Compared with Kucedr's direction

- Little can be checked: no company, no prices, no evidence details. Treat its claims as marketing
  until verified.
- Same pattern as Nexus and OSINT Pilot: agents over many tools in a cloud service. None of them
  puts the work on the investigator's machine.
