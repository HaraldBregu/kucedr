# Maltego and Hunchly

Research note for the [product direction](../PRODUCT.md). Read 2026-11-25 from the sources at the end.
"Not stated" means the pages read did not say.

## At a glance

- **Company:** Maltego Technologies GmbH, Munich, Germany. CEO Philip Mayrhofer.
- **Age:** Maltego has supported investigations "since 2008"; a software directory lists the
  current company as founded in 2017.
- **Ownership:** Charlesbank Capital Partners bought Maltego from Maxburg Capital Partners in a
  deal announced on 18 April 2023, reported as about $100 million.
- **Acquisitions:** PublicSonar and Social Network Harvester (2024), Hunchly (May 2025).
- **Customers (claimed):** "2K+ government organizations", "4K+ private companies" and "60%+ of
  the Dow 30 companies". The Hunchly announcement names the FBI and INTERPOL.

## Mission

- **Maltego:** to build an ecosystem of tools, data and people that lets every investigator
  discover the truth (2024 announcement); with Hunchly, "to empower investigators with tools that
  enhance efficiency and effectiveness".
- **Hunchly:** "To empower people who pursue the truth by giving them the tools to find and
  preserve it amid today's accelerating torrent of information."

## What they sell

From the product list on the pricing page, dated January 2026:

- **Graph (Desktop):** visual link analysis over open and commercial data; the classic Maltego.
- **Graph (Browser):** link analysis in the browser with an AI Assistant; "available soon".
- **Search:** quick lookups from an email, alias, phone number, name or domain.
- **Monitor:** real-time social media monitoring with sentiment analysis and AI assistance.
- **Evidence:** anonymous collection and preservation of social media evidence through research
  accounts ("sock puppets").
- **Maltego One:** a person-of-interest platform combining Graph (Browser) and Search.
- **Data Pass and connectors:** curated open and commercial data, and 100+ integrations through
  the Transform Hub.
- **Cases and Admin:** cloud case storage, user and billing administration.
- **Hunchly:** automatic capture and archiving of web pages with metadata and chain of custody.
- **Services:** Maltego Academy training, onboarding, support, account management and custom
  services.

## Prices

Maltego, in euros, from the pricing page:

- **Basic:** free; Graph Community Edition; 200 credits.
- **Basic+:** free for users invited after registering with a government or organisation email;
  1,000 credits; Maltego One.
- **Entry:** €3,000 a year; 10,000 credits; includes Hunchly. Users who fail vetting get "Entry
  Lite", price not stated.
- **Professional:** €7,500 a year, "up to 5 users (billed per seat)"; 20,000 or 40,000 credits.
- **Enterprise:** contact sales.

Third-party listings disagree widely, from about $999 to €5,000 per user a year; the pricing page
is the reference.

Hunchly, from its pricing page:

- **Hunchly Free:** no credit card required; a 30-day trial is also mentioned.
- **Classic:** €149 or $169 a year. Stores everything locally and works offline.
- **Cloud:** contact sales, by converting a Classic licence.
- **Several licences:** bulk discounts through sales.
- Also included in Maltego's Entry plan and above.

## Evidence and AI

- Hunchly captures every page an investigator visits into a case, with metadata, a cryptographic
  hash and a chain of custody. Dark River Systems launched it in 2015; Sapper Labs later backed it
  and the two merged as Sapper Labs Group in 2022, before Maltego bought it in May 2025.
- Maltego vets accounts: a government or organisation email unlocks Basic+, and users who fail
  vetting are offered Entry Lite.
- AI: Graph (Browser) has an AI Assistant and Monitor has "AI assistance". None is stated for
  Hunchly.

## Compared with Kucedr's direction

- Maltego is the incumbent platform: data integrations, link graphs, monitoring and capture under
  one account, sold to governments and large companies. Kucedr will not match its data catalogue.
- Hunchly is the closest single feature: automatic capture into cases, local storage, $169 a year,
  no agent and no AI.
- Room for Kucedr: an agent that browses and cross-checks, with Hunchly-style capture built in, on
  the user's machine and with the user's model, priced near Hunchly rather than near Maltego's
  €3,000 Entry plan.
