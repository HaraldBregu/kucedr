# Xapien

Research note for the [company-brief direction](../RESEARCH.md). Read 2026-11-25 from the sources at the end.
"Not stated" means the pages read did not say.

## At a glance

- **Company:** Digital Insight Technologies Ltd; Xapien is its registered trademark. Location not
  stated on the pages read; the about page mentions a planned office in Boston.
- **People:** founded in 2018 by Dan Secretan and Shaun O'Mahony (CTO). Chris Green has been CEO
  since 2022; Chris Pilling is chairman.
- **Funding:** Spectrum Equity led a $56 million investment in 2026; YFM Equity Partners and angel
  investors (from 2019) are also listed.
- **Product:** an AI due-diligence platform that writes a sourced report on a person or organisation
  from "a name and some context".
- **Customers:** Dow Jones Risk & Compliance, Pinsent Masons, University of Cambridge, ClientEarth
  and Zurich appear as users or case studies; Mintz Group appears as a logo.

## Mission

> "Xapien is redefining how the world manages risk in business relationships", "establishing a new
> standard for due diligence, without compromise." (about page)

## What they sell

- **Reports:** research across licensed datasets, official registries and the "Entire indexed
  internet + any data set", with a resolution engine for "false positive removal", PEP, sanctions
  and watchlist screening, international corporate records and 200+ languages.
- **Ask Xapien:** "dynamic in-report research" on a finished report.
- **Configuration:** a report template library, configurable templates and risk framework, custom
  branding, bulk upload, API access and prebuilt integrations.

## Prices

No prices published. The plans page lists three 12-month plans, metered by reports, each with
unlimited users and a "Discuss pricing" button:

- **Base:** "From 250 reports/year".
- **Pro:** "From 650 reports/year".
- **Enterprise:** "From 3000 reports/year".
- **Not-for-profit plans:** for qualifying organisations; the FAQ also mentions discounts for
  universities.
- **Allowance rules (help centre):** going "slightly over" the allowance is not charged; failed
  reports and reruns caused by outages do not count; regenerating a report does not count.

A G-Cloud search for "xapien" returned no services. G2 blocked fetching, so its listing was not read.

## Evidence and AI

- "Every assertion is fully sourced"; outputs are "fully sourced down to sentence level". A 2023
  company post says statements trace "down to a sentence and sometimes word or phrase level".
- Method (same post): text is split into sentence fragments, encoded as vectors and linked in a
  knowledge graph; an LLM summarises each report section, inside "non-generative safeguards".
- The FAQ says Xapien uses "over 20 different AI models" and Google Translate; LLM providers are not
  named.
- Saved copies of sources and hashes: not stated.
- Export: PDF only in the help centre, "a self-contained file you can send anywhere". View-only share
  links "open without a Xapien account".
- Hosting: "processed on our servers and is encrypted when at rest"; location not stated.

## Compared with Kucedr's direction

- Xapien automates the part of the direction where AI drafts the brief: a report written from public
  and licensed sources, with each sentence traceable to where it came from.
- The pages read do not say it keeps copies of what it read. In the research's changed-website test,
  the reviewer would follow a link to the live page.
- Price signal: none published. The smallest plan is 250 reports a year, which describes a
  compliance or onboarding team rather than a firm writing a few bespoke briefs a month.
