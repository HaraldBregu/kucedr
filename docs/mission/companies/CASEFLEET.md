# Casefleet

Research note for the [company-brief direction](../RESEARCH.md). Read 2026-11-25 from the sources at the end.
"Not stated" means the pages read did not say.

## At a glance

- **Company:** LAWPRCT, Inc., Atlanta, Georgia, USA. Founding year not stated; the site says
  "Helping to win cases since 2016".
- **People:** not stated.
- **Product:** Casefleet, AI case management software for legal teams, used in the browser.
- **Customers:** litigators and legal teams (employment, criminal, personal injury, workers' comp
  and medical chronologies). The site also lists journalism and writing; testimonials come from
  attorneys and paralegals.
- **Not a research tool:** Casey's search "doesn't pull from outside the case". It works on the
  documents a team uploads.

## Mission

No mission statement on the pages read. The closest line is "A better way to build cases" (home
page).

## What they sell

- **Fact chronologies:** "Every fact linked to its source", with a timeline maker, document review
  and full-text search.
- **Casey, AI assistant:** answers questions "with citations that link directly to source
  material", drafts letters, motions and discovery, and proposes facts, issues and tasks. "Every
  change to your case data is proposed first for your review and approval."
- **Agentic Workflows:** multi-step requests shown as a checklist "visible the whole time"; several
  agents can run on one case, each with its own approval queue.
- **Document Intelligence:** summaries, semantic indexing, named entity recognition, bulk tagging
  and renaming.
- **Reports:** Word, PDF and Excel reports, such as facts by issue, entities with facts and a case
  archive bundle.

## Prices

From the pricing page, in dollars:

- **Advanced AI:** $140 per user per month. "Pay annually (save 22%)" is offered; the annual price
  is not shown. 100,000 AI credits per user per month, then $0.001 per credit. 5 GB of storage per
  account, then $10 per GB per month. Daily upload limits apply; bulk uploads are an add-on.
- **Enterprise:** custom quote; single sign-on, data migration, priority support and custom privacy
  agreements.
- **Trial:** 14 days, no credit card, limited to 50 documents per case.

## Evidence and AI

- Citations only work "for documents processed with Document Intelligence".
- Word reports can be downloaded "with hyperlinked documents" as a `documents.zip` holding the
  report and a `files` folder. Each link opens the cited document at page 1, and links break if the
  files are moved or renamed. Source files are provided "as originally uploaded".
- Web capture and document hashing: not stated. The security page mentions hashing only for
  passwords.
- Hosting: "All of Casefleet's infrastructure is hosted on Amazon Web Services"; region not stated.
  Encryption in transit and at rest, and audit logging.
- Models: not stated. "No model training: Data not used for AI training."

## Compared with Kucedr's direction

- Casefleet already sells the reviewer step the research asks for, in legal work: the AI proposes
  a fact with a citation, a person approves, edits or rejects it, and the exported Word report
  links each citation to its file.
- It has no collection step on the pages read. For the changed-website test in the research, the
  analyst would have to save the page elsewhere and upload it before Casefleet could cite it.
- Price signal: $140 per user per month for cloud-only AI case work on AWS. Kucedr's case for a
  boutique firm would be capture on the analyst's machine, the firm's own model and a brief rather
  than a litigation chronology.
