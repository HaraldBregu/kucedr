# Authentic8 (Silo)

Research note for the [product direction](../PRODUCT.md). Read 2026-11-25 from the sources at the end.
"Not stated" means the pages read did not say.

## At a glance

- **Company:** Authentic8, Redwood City, California, USA.
- **Founded:** 2010, by Ramesh Rajagopal (CEO) and Scott Petry (Executive Chairman).
- **Size and funding:** data aggregators list about 126 to 128 employees in autumn 2026 and about
  $12 million raised over three rounds. The company's own pages do not say.
- **Customers (claimed):** "750+ customers across the world's leading government and commercial
  intelligence teams" and "tens of thousands of analysts"; US and allied government agencies and
  Fortune 2000 companies.
- **Certifications (claimed):** ten, including FedRAMP and SOC 2.
- **Recognition:** its blog reports Silo Workspace was named OSINT Tech Innovation of the Year
  (June 2026).

## Mission

> "Today, Silo is a fully centralized investigative platform, empowering organizations to engage
> directly without exposure in pursuit of critical intelligence."

## What they sell

- **Silo Workspace:** a browser that runs in Authentic8's cloud, so web code never reaches the
  analyst's machine. Nothing to install.
- **Managed attribution:** "700+ owned egress points across 32+ countries" set the analyst's
  apparent location, and browser and platform settings (language, time zone, keyboard, user
  agent) shape their digital fingerprint.
- **Silo Nexus AI:** an assistant included in every tier, up to 10 prompts a day; unlimited use on
  enterprise terms.
- **Silo Marketplace:** approved partner tools and extensions inside Silo.
- **Records:** "a tamper-evident record of who did what". Capture formats and hashing are not
  stated.

## Prices

Per user per year, in US dollars, from the pricing page:

- **Trial:** 30 days free, with limited egress nodes.
- **Local:** $1,450, one region.
- **Multi-Region:** $2,450, two regions.
- **Global:** $3,450, all regions.

## Compared with Kucedr's direction

- Silo is the standard for hiding the investigator: owned exit points in 32+ countries,
  fingerprint control, browsing isolated in the cloud, government certifications. Kucedr's Tor and
  proxy tabs do not hide the browser's fingerprint and should not be sold as an alternative.
- At $1,450 or more per user a year, and cloud only, Silo is out of reach for freelancers and
  small newsrooms. That is the gap a local desk can fill.
- AI is an add-on in Silo (10 prompts a day), not the way the work gets done.
- Silo Nexus AI is unrelated to Next Sight's [Nexus](NEXUS.md).
