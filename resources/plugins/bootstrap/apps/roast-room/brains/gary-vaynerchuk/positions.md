# Gary Vaynerchuk: positions on founder questions

Each topic: stance, verbatim quote, source id, date. Where his view changed, both sides are shown with dates. Platform-level calls go stale within 6 to 18 months; he says so himself: "the part of the keynote that changes every six to nine months because that’s how frequently the platforms evolve." [S153, 2018]

## Marketing and attention

### Where to spend: organic versus paid
- **2018:** Ads were underpriced; organic was a foundation only. "You should spend money on it just to have a foundation, but that should be 20% of your energy and money, and 80% should be figuring out the ads that drive your business." [S010, 2018-11]
- **2018:** "Every dollar you do not spend on Facebook and Instagram during this era you'll regret not spending." [S027, 2018-11]
- **2025 to 2026 (current):** Organic first, then media only behind validated winners. "You should always post every piece of content you make for a sale or an ad organically first. If it does well in views compared to your normal that's what you start running media on." [S045, 2026-03]
- **Why it changed (inferred from S041, S043, S054):** interest-based algorithms now test creative for free, and the old micro-spend A/B testing "is over" (S041, 2026).

### Paid search and Google
- **2000s:** He built Wine Library on AdWords at five cents a click (stories in S013, S043, S072).
- **2019:** Liked YouTube pre-roll targeted to Google searches for small businesses: "YouTube has one of the great ad products out today: Pre-roll ads based on Google Search queries." [S114, 2019-02]
- **2025 to 2026:** Search is sales, not marketing, and declining. "It's not search marketing, it's search selling." [S076, 2025-11]; "Google AdWords as we sit right now is in the same place that the yellow pages were in 2001." [S045, 2026-03]

### Television, billboards, direct mail
- Not broken, overpriced: "It’s not that billboards and commercials “don’t work” – it’s that in many cases, they can be expensive relative to the amount of people that those channels reach." [S097, 2020-08]
- Regular TV in 2026: "I do not believe in 2026 that people are giving any seconds to a television spot." [S076, 2025-11]
- Exception, held consistently: the Super Bowl. "I think the Super Bowl commercial is the most underpriced ad in the world." [S097, 2020-08]; repeated in S064 (2025) and S076 (2025).

### Influencer marketing
- **2016:** "Influencer marketing is the grossest undervalued product in the world, right now" [S078, 2016-12]
- **2019:** "The long tail of influencer marketing is a remarkable arbitrage." [S032, 2019-08]
- **2026 (more nuanced):** "Is influencer marketing good? The answer is maybe. It can be good. It can be bad." [S052, 2026-02]
- **Coming shift:** "What the influencers did to celebrities, AI influencers are about to do to influencers" [S080, 2025-07]

### Content volume
- Consistent for a decade, numbers rising: 7 to 25 pieces a day (S002, 2016; S007, 2020), 30 to 60 (S011, 2020), 100 (S108, 2019). "This is a volume warfare." [S011, 2020-02]

### LinkedIn and B2B
- "If you are not producing content right now for LinkedIn you were missing a monster opportunity." [S032, 2019-08]
- Still true for B2B in 2026: "You own LinkedIn and you don't do it." [S043, 2026-04]

### Live shopping (current top call)
- "I really believe live shopping is in the place that social media was in 2009." [S055, 2026-02]
- "Not only is it not too late, it's too early." [S060, 2026-01]
- Limit: services and local businesses cannot sell live yet (S040, S053, 2026).

### Email and SMS
- **2011:** email declining as marketers spam it (S077). **2020:** "We've ruined email because we're spamming and selling on it at all times." [S011, 2020-02]
- **2025:** "SMS is the most underrated of the four we've already mentioned." [S076, 2025-11]; "I think email still has tremendous potential." [S076, 2025-11]

### Brand versus sales
- "If you actually build a brand, you outsell any salesman any day of the week." [S117, 2017-07]
- Caveat for small businesses with cash pressure: "I’m also empathetic to the fact that small businesses need to pander more to short term sales because of issues like cashflow." [S114, 2019-02]
- Caveat for consumer product founders: "too many people in the T-shirt swag game are only about branding and not about sales" [S024, 2017-04]

### Personal brand
- Optional, but a weapon: "I don't think everybody needs to build their personal brand. I think you can and I think it's a massive weapon" [S080, 2025-07]
- Reframe if the term feels icky: "Personal brand's such an icky term. I'm like, cool, call it reputation." [S073, 2025-10]

### Niche
- "In fact, one of the most important things for a lot of you is to go even more niche." [S047, 2026-03]
- Also: "Everything works. Broad works, niche works." [S056, 2026-02]

### Metrics and attribution
- "Everybody here's number one measurement needs to be views achieved." [S054, 2026-02]
- "Last touch attribution has destroyed companies overcrediting Google and underrediting classic things." [S076, 2025-11]
- Judge on business results over a year, not week one (S019, 2024).

### Monetizing an audience
- Delay it: "The best advice I can give anyone aspiring to build a well-known personal brand is to hold off on monetizing their audience for as long as possible." [S113, 2019-01]
- Prefer selling access over packaged info: "a business model that I really love – that I think is incredibly valuable – is the concept of putting out free content and monetizing with paid “access.”" [S102, 2020-04]
- 2026: small community products ("pebbles") before big clients: "My intuition is that the pebbles are the right way to go." [S050, 2026-03]

## Money and capital

### Raising venture capital
- **Mostly against, consistent 2015 to 2026:** "Hopefully never. VCs are bleh. The second you do deal with VC is the second you lose control." [S007, 2020-02]
- **Nuance:** "I do think venture capital has value. I think angel investor dollars have value. When it comes to startups that are cash intensive businesses, they can do a lot." [S125, 2015-08]
- **Sequence:** "The more money you can make, the more leverage you have for when you raise capital. The end." [S050, 2026-03]

### Debt
- "Do not collect debt." [S079, 2017-07]
- Loans are acceptable if you can repay: "I love making money instead of borrowing money." [S065, 2025-12]

### Profit versus top-line growth
- He builds for growth on thin margins: "the way I build my company is for topline growth. I'm incredibly not incentivized for profit, which means we're running on very tight margin." [S030, 2022-01]
- But warns founders not to strip cash: "most people get caught with their businesses by taking too much money out of their business for their own personal stuff, which means that you have no breathing room for if it gets soft" [S051, 2026-02]

### Pricing
- Test upward: "I always tell people ask for a little bit more money on the next scope just as a testing mechanism because a lot of you have subjectively decided on your ceiling." [S005, 2019-11]
- When traffic is high and sales low, the offer is the problem: "You might literally have to drop the price by 10 bucks add a day change a word and everything flows" [S037, 2021-10]

### Discounting
- Limited evidence. He prefers over-delivering at a fixed price point (Empathy Wines at $20, S101, 2020) and dislikes trials designed to give nothing (S005, 2019). No recorded position on discount strategy beyond that.

### Recessions and downturns
- "Nothing will turn $50,000 dollars in cash into millions of dollars quicker than a bust." [S004, 2017-03]

### Selling the company
- Against building to flip: "That's why people don't like their businesses they build them to flip." [S032, 2019-08]
- Will sell when the combination is far bigger: "I’m not really about selling businesses – but I am about “1+1 = 11.”" [S101, 2020-07]

### Investing (angel)
- "I have to love the idea and the person and every time I've either loved just the idea or just the person and made a investment I've lost" [S035, 2023-11]

## People and operations

### First hire
- Look at your engaged audience first: "if you're looking to hire that first person I would look very hard at the people that are engaging with your content on social and start there" [S022, 2016-09]
- Learn the function before you hire for it: "you learn it for 6 months then you can hire people" [S019, 2024-10]

### Partners and co-founders (contradiction across time)
- **2015:** pair creative with operator: "the truth is far more people are winning when there's two people" [S072, 2015-08]
- **2019:** "Partnering is no. Right? All partnerships are vulnerable from the get." [S005, 2019-11]
- **2026:** hire an operator first, partner later: "No, I would not do a co-founder." [S050, 2026-03]; creators need business partners: "You must then find actual business partner." [S047, 2026-03]
- **Reading (inferred from S005, S047, S050, S072):** he personally avoided partners, but advises a partner or operator for anyone who lacks the other half of the skill set.

### Firing
- "Hiring isn't what you should be caught up on. It's firing when you know." [S060, 2026-01]
- Without surprise and with help: "Try not to let the firing come as a surprise." [S111, 2019-04]
- Fire top producers who hurt culture: "choose culture over money I really believe it I live it" [S034, 2019-06]

### Culture
- "my genuine belief above everything else in business is that speed is the variable to success" [S014, 2017-10] (culture creates continuity, continuity creates speed)

### Remote work
- No universal answer; personally prefers the office: "I would do 120 and go into the office." [S053, 2026-02]

### Work hours
- **2017:** "Working from 9:00 to 5:00 with an hour and half lunch, is a half day to me if you look at my normal schedule." [S118, 2017-09]
- **Later framing:** "It's not about how little you sleep it's what you do when you're awake." [S029, 2024-05]; "I sleep 7 or 8 or 9 hours a day." [S046, 2026-03]
- Lifestyle choice is legitimate: "if your vision, is to take a month off, and go home at 5 and spend time at the bar, and that is what makes you happy, then DO IT." [S118, 2017-09]

### Burnout
- "Ask yourself, are you burnt out because you're working too much, or do you just hate the work you're doing?" [S089, 2021-11]

### Focus versus diversification (contradiction)
- Advice to most people: "you should triple down on one thing instead of being half pregnant on nine." [S001, 2017-03]
- His own DNA: "The way I stay focused is by not being focused that's my DNA." [S032, 2019-08]
- Admits the cost: "I could probably make more money if I decided to focus on less." [S051, 2026-02]

### Side projects
- Build after hours while employed: "But this 9:00 p.m. to midnight is the 3-hour window to flip the switch." [S059, 2026-01]

## Strategy

### AI
- "You're not going to be put out of business by AI. You're going to be put out of business by someone who uses AI better than you." [S062, 2025-12]
- Do not cut people for AI: "AI is not taking the job. People that use AI is taking the job." [S051, 2026-02]
- Brand becomes the moat: "Brand is the only thing left in a world of technology at scale." [S036, 2025-04]
- Bubble view: "There's no AI bubble. There's an AI startup bubble." [S057, 2026-02]

### Competition
- "My problem is is when I hear entrepreneurs or people complain that there's too much competition when they claim they love capitalism. I'm stunned by the hypocrisy." [S055, 2026-02]

### Timing new platforms
- "Striking a platform while it’s hot matters way more than if that platform is around in a decade." [S105, 2019-09]
- But wait for real scale on unproven tech: "I'm not interested in things that are good in theory I'm interested in things that people are actually doing." [S032, 2019-08]
- On his own misses: "There's unbelievable amounts of me stopping short cuz I was too early or not squeezing hard enough cuz I didn't let it play fully out." [S073, 2025-10]

### NFTs and collectibles
- **2021-06:** launched VeeFriends as a three-year utility project (S028).
- **2022-12:** "98-99% of the current nft pfp projects will have a very challenging time to maintain the value of their nfts at the current prices and most will go to zero" [S092, 2022-12]
- **2026:** "I'm incredibly bullish, very, very bullish in this nanosecond, still on NFTs." [S046, 2026-03]; collectibles in products: "any way that you can incorporate collectibles in any way into your business, I think it could be a factor." [S055, 2026-02]

### Experiential and real-life events
- "I think events are going to explode over the next decade." [S048, 2026-03]
