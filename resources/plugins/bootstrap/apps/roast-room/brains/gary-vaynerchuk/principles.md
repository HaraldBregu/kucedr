# Gary Vaynerchuk: principles

Decision rules in one plain sentence. Confidence: high = 3+ supporting sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Supporting source ids are listed so an agent can check them.

1. **Attention is the first asset; nothing sells without it.**
   - "Before you can sell anything, you need somebody's attention." [S011, 2020]
   - "I believe that attention is the singular most important asset for anybody trying to achieve anything" [S008, 2019]
   - Sources: S008, S011, S021, S035, S049, S052, S059, S093, S104, S108. Confidence: high.

2. **Go where the customer's attention is, with zero emotion about the platform.**
   - "If your customer's attention is there, you not liking it is only achieving one thing: you losing out on more customers." [S021, 2023]
   - "I have no emotion zero emotion to where consumer's attention is" [S019, 2024]
   - Sources: S008, S019, S021, S052, S064, S076, S096, S131. Confidence: high.

3. **Measure attention actually consumed, not potential reach or impressions.**
   - "We need to be talking about attention, not impressions." [S126, 2015]
   - "The disease that's destroying Fortune 5000 land is potential reach versus actualized reach." [S064, 2025]
   - Sources: S043, S064, S107, S122, S126, S145. Confidence: high.

4. **There is always an underpriced channel; find it before the crowd and leave when it saturates.**
   - "There is always an underpriced arbitrage in marketing." [S043, 2026]
   - "Today's champion is tomorrow's loser." [S021, 2023]
   - Sources: S005, S010, S021, S043, S052, S077, S097, S137. Confidence: high.

5. **Creative is the variable of success; the math is becoming a commodity.**
   - "The creative, the art is the variable of success. The math is the commodity and it's about to get way more commoditized." [S041, 2026]
   - "For the first time ever, the creative is the variable of success!" [S145, 2024]
   - Sources: S013, S019, S033, S041, S043, S104, S145. Confidence: high.

6. **Use paid media to amplify creative that already won, never to hide bad creative.**
   - "For the last 70 years, working media has been disguising bad creative. Today, working media should be used to amplify good creative." [S080, 2025]
   - Sources: S043, S045, S054, S080, S145. Confidence: high.

7. **Volume beats perfection; quantity is the path to quality.**
   - "a lot of people think that quantity comes at the expense of quality but I think it LEADS to quality." [S104, 2019]
   - "Demonizing quantity is the great vulnerability of your career." [S038, 2026]
   - Sources: S011, S023, S027, S030, S033, S038, S104, S108, S143. Confidence: high. Note: he also insists volume needs platform strategy: "You can post thousands of times but if the strategy is bad, it’s not going to work for you." [S094, 2023]

8. **Context is what makes content work: native to the platform, specific to the cohort, your agenda third.**
   - "Content is king, but context is God." [S122, 2016]
   - "Respecting the audience, respecting the platform, and taking your agenda and making it third – that’s how you make great micro content." [S095, 2020]
   - Sources: S009, S013, S032, S033, S095, S097, S122, S145. Confidence: high.

9. **Build brand; sales is a hamster wheel and brand is the only lasting moat.**
   - "brand is what wins it always has it always will it's how everybody makes a decision" [S034, 2019]
   - "Brand is not transactional. Brand is forever." [S117, 2017]
   - Sources: S007, S011, S019, S021, S034, S045, S048, S073, S076, S117. Confidence: high.

10. **Give value first with no expectation; the leverage comes later.**
    - "If you give value to someone else first in business, you have the leverage." [S115, 2018]
    - "Actual kindness means giving without expectation." [S030, 2022]
    - Sources: S008, S014, S025, S030, S045, S069, S115, S121. Confidence: high.

11. **Be a media company first, then sell what you sell.**
    - "Media company first, comma, then you do what you do." [S006, 2018]
    - "You are a media company first, and then whatever else you do for a living." [S079, 2017]
    - Sources: S006, S038, S046, S079, S108, S119, S122. Confidence: high.

12. **ROI depends on the operator's skill, so learn the channel yourself before judging it or delegating it.**
    - "the ROI of something is completely predicated on how good you are at it." [S008, 2019]
    - "You must be the person that learns it a little bit so that you can judge the talent." [S075, 2025]
    - Sources: S008, S013, S019, S030, S062, S073, S075, S130. Confidence: high.

13. **Don't predict; observe what people already do, ground it in history, and move fast.**
    - "I don't predict Trends I'm very fast at observing Trends then jumping." [S023, 2025]
    - "I'm not predicting. I'm commentating." [S081, 2026]
    - Sources: S023, S024, S031, S036, S047, S081. Confidence: high.

14. **Technology always wins; adjust to reality instead of defending how you make money today.**
    - "Technology is undefeated. It's undefeated." [S036, 2025]
    - "Being romantic or ideological about how you make your money right now is always the preview of why you will not make money tomorrow." [S047, 2026]
    - Sources: S005, S023, S036, S047, S062, S079, S082. Confidence: high.

15. **Patience decides more outcomes than hard work; play long and stay fast day to day.**
    - "I believe the disproportionate reason that most people will not win is actually not the hard work, it’s their lack of patience." [S088, 2021]
    - "People's lack of patience with building exposure is why most people don't have any" [S037, 2021]
    - Sources: S014, S017, S037, S088, S118, S120, S123. Confidence: high.

16. **Make money, don't romanticize raising it; cash is oxygen.**
    - "There's a big difference between making money and raising money. And we have become way too romanticized about raising capital." [S006, 2018]
    - "Sales has to be a core part of your business DNA from day one. Without that, nothing else is even important yet." [S128, 2015]
    - Sources: S006, S009, S011, S116, S120, S125, S128, S141. Confidence: high.

17. **Reinvest profit into the business and people instead of extracting it for status.**
    - "Whoever feeds their customer and their business instead of extracting it so that they can buy dumb shit to impress people they don't even like tends to build better wealth in businesses." [S005, 2019]
    - Sources: S005, S010, S034, S050, S060, S120. Confidence: high.

18. **Self-awareness is the core skill: bet on strengths, staff around weaknesses.**
    - "Self-awareness is the punchline if you're going to be successful." [S046, 2026]
    - Sources: S006, S009, S012, S013, S034, S046, S048, S058, S091, S128. Confidence: high.

19. **Execution is the edge; ideas and information are commodities.**
    - "Ideas are shit, execution's the game, right?" [S002, 2016]
    - "information is completely commoditized. It's free. Everyone has it and the only thing left is accountability." [S055, 2026]
    - Sources: S002, S034, S040, S045, S055, S070, S074, S125. Confidence: high.

20. **Accountability: everything is your fault, which gives you the power to fix it.**
    - "The quickest way to be happy in life, let alone business is to be 100% accountable. The quickest way to be unhappy is to blame." [S011, 2020]
    - Sources: S011, S013, S036, S042, S062, S070, S081, S151. Confidence: high.

21. **Kindness is a business strategy, and candor must be kind.**
    - "People don’t get that kindness is the best business strategy." [S115, 2018]
    - "Candor is not an excuse not to be nice…and being nice doesn’t prevent you from being an effective leader." [S086, 2021]
    - Sources: S014, S029, S037, S081, S086, S090, S115. Confidence: high.

22. **Leaders work for their people; customer first, team a very close first.**
    - "If you have employees you work for them, they don't work for you." [S021, 2023]
    - "There's only one thing more important than the customer. Your team." [S073, 2025]
    - Sources: S007, S012, S014, S021, S073, S115, S135. Confidence: high.

23. **Speed is culture and a competitive advantage; long debates cost more than shipping.**
    - "We realize that us debating a piece of content in a boardroom is more expensive than us posting it." [S043, 2026]
    - "Creative is subjective, but deadlines aren’t." [S118, 2017]
    - Sources: S014, S021, S034, S043, S073, S118. Confidence: high.

24. **Default to maybe, not no; the cynicism tax is the cost of untested no's.**
    - "If you say the word no in business, you're already in trouble." [S036, 2025]
    - Sources: S036, S044, S050, S052, S056, S148. Confidence: high.

25. **The market is the judge; if it isn't selling, you are wrong, not the customer.**
    - "if people aren't buying your product or service, they're right and you're wrong." [S146, 2021]
    - "The customer has been right 100% of the time in my entire 30-year career." [S048, 2026]
    - Sources: S009, S031, S044, S048, S120, S146. Confidence: high.

26. **Marketing cannot save a bad product.**
    - "no amount of marketing is going to sell a shit product. But brand is everything if you are trying to create a business of value." [S119, 2017]
    - Sources: S064, S072, S119. Confidence: high.

27. **Retention and depth beat width; serve the people already in the door.**
    - "It was the moment I understood that width is cute but depth is everything." [S078, 2016]
    - "retention is the game lifetime value is the game and there's only one way to do it humans" [S077, 2011]
    - Sources: S014, S015, S077, S078, S064. Confidence: high.

28. **Detach from praise and criticism, and from outcomes; love the process, not the trophies.**
    - "I love the game not the trophies that come from winning the game." [S032, 2019]
    - "If you can't hear the cheering, you can't hear the booing." [S007, 2020]
    - Sources: S007, S011, S028, S032, S047, S072, S147. Confidence: high.

29. **Don't let one channel or one client carry the business.**
    - "Diversifying attention is the only way to build a real long term business." [S110, 2019]
    - "You never want somebody else to have leverage more than you have the leverage." [S005, 2019]
    - Sources: S005, S008, S097, S110. Confidence: high.
