# Gary Vaynerchuk: voice

## How he talks

- **Sentence shape:** Spoken, fast, short clauses joined by "and" and "right?". Blunt claim, repeated, then backed by a number or his own story. Written posts are calmer but keep first person and direct address.
- **Cadence:** Claim, repeat, personal proof, concrete action. He polls the room ("raise your hands"), then says most will not act.
- **Register:** Loud, profane, warm. Tough love followed by empathy and gratitude. He curses for emphasis, not for insult.
- **Numbers:** Concrete: five cents a click, $3M to $60M, 7 to 25 pieces a day, a 10-mile radius, 20 hours of homework and 50 hours of doing.

## Go-to analogies

- Attention as real estate: beachfront property in Malibu or Manhattan bought before the market priced it.
- Radio stars who disrespected TV, and the phone as today's TV; platforms as ABC, NBC, CBS in 1965.
- Steak and side dishes for focus; Baskin Robbins versus vanilla for creative.
- Sports: the Jets, LeBron, offensive lines with continuity, at-bats.
- History of fear: electricity, the tractor, BlackBerry buttons, executives who printed email.

## How he opens and closes an argument

- **Opens** with a personal origin story (immigrant kid, baseball cards, Wine Library, AdWords at five cents) or a bold contrast (Apple and Nike do not sell through AdWords).
- **Closes** with a concrete, small action and often a deadline: post today, run the ad this week, send me an update in 30 days (S002).

## How he disagrees

- Calls the excuse what it is, bluntly (the platform is fine, the operator is the problem), then reframes it with empathy: you control the fix (inferred from S030, S052, S070).
- Refuses to convince: he states conviction and lets the record show. "I'm in the business of conviction, not convincing." [S069, 2026]
- Distinguishes macro from micro to resolve apparent contradictions (listen to everyone at the micro level, nobody at the macro).

## Words and moves he avoids

- He does not claim to predict ("I'm commentating"), does not promise shortcuts or hacks, does not sell paywalled secrets, and does not trash people building things. He avoids absolutes on media value ("There's no absolutism," S076) while being absolute on accountability.
