# Principles: Gino Wickman

24 decision rules. "Sources" is the number of corpus sources whose notes carry the rule (approximate, from the digest). Confidence: high = 3 or more of Gino's own sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

## Operating

1. **Treat problems as symptoms; fix the root component.** Most of what founders call "a dozen problems" traces back to one or two of the Six Key Components.
   - "go to the root of their issue and solve it there 136 issues just kind of fell away" (S043)
   - "by going to the root and solving them, you save unbelievable amounts of time, pain, and suffering." (S108)
   - Sources: 8. Confidence: high.

2. **Get the vision out of the founder's head and the leadership team 100% on the same page before pushing execution.**
   - "you've got to get your vision out of your head and on paper you owe it to your customers and clients you owe it to your vendors you owe it to your employees" (S032)
   - Sources: 10. Confidence: high.

3. **Structure first, people second.** Draw the seats for where the company is going, then decide who sits in them.
   - "Most of the problems lie in the wrong structure and the wrong people in the wrong seats." (S025)
   - "Remember, always look at structure first and people second." (S109)
   - Sources: 8. Confidence: high.

4. **Right people in the right seats is the biggest lever once vision and structure are clear, and people moves should not wait.**
   - "the number one impact on an organization’s growth, once the vision is clear and the structure is right, comes from getting the right people in the right seats." (S121)
   - "Anyone that doesn't you need to help them go somewhere else." (S029)
   - Sources: 12. Confidence: high.

5. **Run on one operating system, and anyone at the top who will not commit to it has to go.**
   - "You cannot build a great company on multiple operating systems. You must choose one." (S030)
   - "When I see that, I confront it. And if they don't cave, they're gone." (S030)
   - Sources: 7. Confidence: high.

6. **Keep it simple: the 20% of tools that get 80% of the results, and no theory.**
   - "I just take the 20/80 approach to the world and figure out the 20% that will get a company 80% of the results" (S117)
   - "Simple, practical. It does not have to be complex." (S025)
   - Sources: 9. Confidence: high.

7. **Be fanatical about resolution.** An issue is solved only when there is an owner, a To-Do, and real agreement; issues should never linger.
   - "For strong leadership teams, issues never linger." (S094)
   - "One of the purest forms of leadership is the willingness to stay in the room until it’s done." (S091)
   - Sources: 11. Confidence: high.

8. **Open and honest is the foundation; when a team is not honest, the leader is the cause.**
   - "The root of all dysfunction in an organization is a lack of being open and honest." (S038)
   - "If your team isn't open and honest, it's you. It's you." (S033)
   - Sources: 5. Confidence: high.

9. **Execute the basics consistently for years; do not change a working system because it got boring.**
   - "We did the same thing every day, every week, every month, every quarter for 10 years." (S038)
   - "Most successes are due to executing the basics consistently." (S109)
   - Sources: 6. Confidence: high.

10. **Run the business on a handful of weekly, activity-based numbers, not gut feel or a lagging P&L.**
    - "The five to 15 most important activity-based numbers or your business." (S055)
    - Sources: 11. Confidence: high.

11. **Work in a 90-day world.** Re-set three to seven priorities every quarter, because alignment decays within about 90 days.
    - "the 90-day world is coming together every 90 days as a leadership team to avoid the fray and keep focused." (S035)
    - Sources: 9. Confidence: high.

12. **Vision without discipline and accountability is a wish.**
    - "Bringing discipline and accountability into the organization taking the vision down to the ground and making it real." (S064)
    - Sources: 5. Confidence: high.

## Focus and strategy

13. **Stay true to your core; say no to the shiny stuff.** Maximize the sweet spot before looking anywhere else.
    - "i'm a fanatic about focus and so yes there's the tiny percent of stories you hear about the entrepreneur that started eight different businesses but it's an anomaly" (S063)
    - "he who chase two rabbits catch none so so pick one and go" (S085)
    - Sources: 10. Confidence: high.

14. **Pick a narrow target market and keep it, even when experts tell you to broaden it.**
    - "They said please don't put that target market in this book you'll alienate people and I said hallelujah it's staying in the book." (S082)
    - Sources: 6. Confidence: high.

15. **Think in 10-year time frames: take action, but be patient.**
    - "make every decision today in a 10-year time frame you'll make better decisions you'll get there faster" (S032)
    - Sources: 16. Confidence: high.

16. **Charge more than feels comfortable; most companies undercharge out of fear.**
    - "Most companies are under charging for their product or service" (S053)
    - "there's a just a psychological issue with people they're just afraid to charge what they should be charging" (S055)
    - Sources: 6. Confidence: high.

17. **Build only the size of company you are built for; a profitable $5M company is a success.**
    - "Some entrepreneurs should never have more than three employees and some should have 300,000 employees." (S041)
    - "There's no shame in building a $4 million company." (S057)
    - Sources: 8. Confidence: high.

18. **Prepare for the two bad years in good times: six months of cash, and a plan for a 30 to 50 percent revenue hit.**
    - "Do you have six months cash reserves in your personal bank account and in your business bank account?" (S068)
    - Sources: 6. Confidence: high.

## The founder

19. **Every Visionary needs an Integrator; get one as soon as you can afford it or stomach the risk.**
    - "Get an integrator to free you up from all that stuff." (S035)
    - "every true Visionary needs to be counter-balanced with a great Integrator" (S102)
    - Sources: 20. Confidence: high.

20. **Delegate and elevate continuously, or the company grows past you.**
    - "the organization is going to grow right past you and you will get left behind." (S122)
    - "If you catch yourself doing 25 an hour work you've got to treat it like the plague." (S029)
    - Sources: 16. Confidence: high.

21. **Know your 100% and take real time off; rest is a growth lever and a test of whether the business runs without you.**
    - "One of the biggies is seeing if your business can run without you that's the whole reason you own a business." (S029)
    - "I have taken the month of August off every year for the last 25 years." (S058)
    - Sources: 10. Confidence: high.

22. **When a decision is hard, take yourself to the worst case, then put a stake in the ground.** Set a date and time by which you will decide.
    - "When it's really big and scary and you have some time, put a stake in the ground." (S030)
    - Sources: 5. Confidence: high.

23. **Only three options exist for any issue: live with it, end it, or change it.**
    - "The reality is that you only have three choices when it comes to what you are wrestling with." (S123)
    - Sources: 6. Confidence: high.

24. **Decide from love, not fear; know thyself before you decide.**
    - "your thoughts, feelings, emotions and decisions are either coming from love or fear." (S048)
    - Sources: 8. Confidence: high. (Mostly from his post-2021 Shine work; use it on founder-energy questions.)
