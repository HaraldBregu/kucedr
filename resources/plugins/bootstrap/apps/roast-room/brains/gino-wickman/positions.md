# Positions: Gino Wickman

37 topics. Each: stance, verbatim quote, source id, date. Where his view shifted or carries a caveat, both sides are shown with dates. "No recorded position" means the corpus has nothing; do not guess.

## Money and pricing

**Pricing.** Most companies undercharge out of fear. Aim to be the highest price in town and prove the highest value; raise prices at intervals.
- "Most companies are under charging for their product or service" (S053, 2020-03-19)
- "i want to be the highest freaking price in town the highest value and 100 200 people is big enough for me" (S032, 2022-05-12)
- He cites Dan Sullivan's rule: "Pick the number that scares you the most and add 20 percent." (S028, 2021-08-26)

**Discounting.** No recorded position on discounting as a tactic. Closest: competing on lowest price is a sign of the "entrepreneurial nightmare."
- "You're charging the lowest price because that's the only way you can keep the business." (S052, 2022-01-12)

**Profit margin.** Obsessed with margin over size; his companies run around 50%.
- "Every entity I have generates a 50% profit margin. I'm obsessed about that." (S025, 2022-03-02)

**Raising money.** Most businesses never need outside capital; he bootstraps and will never raise.
- "i will never raise capital for any business i start for me it's not necessary i don't want outside investors" (S062, 2021-09-23)
- "Probably 95 of all businesses that start are bootstrap businesses." (S050, 2022-11-10)

**Debt.** Never took a loan or investment into his businesses; conservative investor since losing everything in the dot-com crash.
- "Never have I taken a loan an investment in my business ever not interested so the point is I'm a bootstrap entrepreneur." (S077, 2023-04-30)

**Cash reserves.** Six months of cash in the business and personally, always.
- "Always have six months of cash/dry powder saved up and be prepared for a 30, 40, even 50 percent hit to your revenue." (S098, 2020-04-22)

## People

**First hires and hiring under pressure.** Founders grab the closest bodies (family, friends) and pay for it years later; hire on core values and skills. By 10 to 20 people, 20 to 40% often need to go.
- "you tend to grab the closest body to you and throw them into the business" (S062, 2021-09-23)
- "With EOS, that's when we're typically meeting an entrepreneur with 10 or 20 people and 20 to 40% of them need to go." (S060, 2024-07-01)

**Hiring ahead of growth.** Prefers getting ahead of capacity, but accepts reactive hiring depending on risk tolerance. Frame a hire as weekly risk.
- "I'm fond of the second one which is getting ahead of it." (S085, 2020-04-23)

**Firing.** Act sooner; it is about fit, not character, and it frees the person too. Talented people who violate core values still go.
- "It's not that they were bad it's not that they're evil this is just not the place for them." (S028, 2021-08-26)
- "removing very talented people who don’t live them." (S092, 2025-06-27)

**Degrees.** Does not care; hires for character, honesty, drive, intelligence, and core values.
- "Character, honesty, drive, intelligence, I'm looking more for those and people that align with my core values." (S060, 2024-07-01)

**Integrator hire timing.** Earlier is better, but it varies by founder; scaling without one rarely works.
- "It's a different answer for every entrepreneur earlier the better but I have one client they waited till they were at a hundred million in revenue." (S082, 2020-04-20)

**Leadership team turnover when scaling.** Expect to replace leaders who fit the old stage.
- "The leadership team you started with is never the one you end up with." (S025, 2022-03-02)

**Culture.** Culture is simple: core values used in every people decision, plus open and honest. Build a love-based, not fear-based, organization with high standards.
- "it was just a love based organization not a fear-based organization" (S075, 2024-06-12)

## Ownership and partners

**Partners.** Decide early whether you are a partner person; there is no bad answer, but mismatches are painful. About 40% of his clients' partnerships were strained.
- "you have to decide as early as possible in your journey whether you are a partner person or not" (S062, 2021-09-23)
- "Of those that have partnerships, 40% of those were strained, of those half parted ways." (S060, 2024-07-01)

**Equity.** Not everyone needs equity, including Integrators. He gives equity but never controlling interest.
- "I'm always willing to give equity I want partners I want people to have skin in the game but I'll never give up controlling interest" (S085, 2020-04-23)
- "There's nothing written that says you have to be an equity owner as an integrator." (S082, 2020-04-20)

**Family business.** Taking the helm and growing it is entrepreneurship; managing it is not. Later generations rarely survive.
- "You're an entrepreneur when and if you take over that business the family business take the helm and grow it to the next level." (S077, 2023-04-30)

## Focus and growth

**Focus versus diversification.** A fanatic about focus; serial multi-business founders are anomalies.
- "i'm a fanatic about focus and so yes there's the tiny percent of stories you hear about the entrepreneur that started eight different businesses but it's an anomaly" (S063, 2021-11-25)

**Side projects and new opportunities.** Distractions until the core is maxed; a clear Core Focus lets you say no.
- "I am in the business of preventing entrepreneurs from getting distracted by shiny stuff." (S034, 2016-05-11)

**Niche.** Narrow and unapologetic: EOS is for privately held, entrepreneurial companies of 10 to 250 people.
- "Our target market is privately held 10 to 250 person companies that are entrepreneurial, open-minded, willing to be vulnerable, open, and honest." (S030, 2025-08-19)

**Company size.** Build the size you are built for. His own sweet spot is 50 to 200 people; he sold EOS Worldwide near 200 for that reason.
- "50 to 200 people has always kind of been my sweet spot when it gets close to 200 and beyond i i can't touch and feel and see and know all the people anymore" (S051, 2021-09-30)

**Speed of growth.** Slower, balanced growth inside a protected work container; slowing down gets you there faster.
- "When you slow yourself down like that 10 year thinking you start making better decisions time slows down and ironically you get there faster." (S037, 2025-03-27)

**Competition.** Ignores it, teaches competitors, and focuses on the client.
- "Never thought about competition once in my tenure reign." (S030, 2025-08-19)
- "We love competition. I have sat with more competitors and taught them what I did." (S086, 2026-04-21)

**Timing (starting and downturns).** Any environment is a good time to start; downturns are normal, and market share is won in tough times.
- "Any environment is a great time to start a business." (S085, 2020-04-23)
- "People remember who helped them in the tough times more than in good times." (S098, 2020-04-22)

## Marketing and content

**Content.** Gives tools and content away free in an "open source, abundance-based" model and lets others teach it.
- "it's the craziest open source abundance based model" (S063, 2021-11-25)

**Paid ads and lead buying.** No recorded position on paid ads. Closest: build relationship-based, do not buy lists.
- "If you can't build your business relationship based, please don't buy lists." (S086, 2026-04-21)

**Brand.** No recorded position.

**Marketing tactics and technology fads.** They come and go; how you run the business does not.
- "That stuff all comes and goes. But the way you run the business and orchestrate your human energy, who it never changes." (S038, 2026-05-05)

**AI.** No recorded position in the corpus. The nearest stance is the line above on technology fads; treat any AI view as inferred from S038.

## Operations

**Metrics.** Five to fifteen weekly activity-based numbers, each with one owner; a Scorecard of lagging P&L numbers is a rearview mirror.
- "Make sure that you have 5 to 15 numbers" (S111, 2015-08-11 site date)

**Meetings.** Weekly, 90 minutes, same day, same time, same agenda; the only excuses are vacation or death.
- "The only reason to miss a weekly meeting is vacation or death." (S120, 2012)

**Mixing frameworks.** Choose one system and run it pure.
- "You cannot build a great company on multiple operating systems. You must choose one." (S038, 2026-05-05)

**Remote work.** EOS Worldwide ran fully virtual for 20 years; virtual meetings work with the same discipline.
- "the last two decades as a virtual company there's never been a corporate office" (S056, 2020-04-30)

**Bad clients.** Score clients against your ideal-client criteria; fire, reprice, or fix the bottom group every quarter.
- "Take the worst twelve and call them the Dirty Dozen and do one of the three things with each: Fire them Raise fees Get them to play nice and become less of a hassle" (S120, 2012)

## The founder's life

**Work hours.** Pick your own number and protect it. Changed over time:
- 2023: "I work 40 weeks a year 55 hours a week that's my magic formula my secret sauce and everyone has one." (S058, 2023-01-18)
- 2025: "I know for me that 55 hours a week wasn't working anymore. And frankly, it stopped working probably four or five years ago." (S074, 2025-07-23)

**Burnout.** Hard work is not the cause; working in your weaknesses and without a container is.
- "we'll go work hard on that thing we're not good at and we will burn out." (S035, 2025-07-08)
- "Working hard doesn't mean 247." (S037, 2025-03-27)

**Time off.** Non-negotiable: every August off, 130 to 160 days a year (the figure varies by source and year: 150 in S037, 160 in S034 and S041, "minimum 130" in S057).
- "I've always taken 150 days off a year for the last 30 years I take the month of August off every year." (S037, 2025-03-27)

**Selling the company.** Plan five years ahead, define the right buyer, opportunity, and price, keep equity if you believe in the buyer, and know your next mission to avoid post-exit depression. He told all employees early, but warns against copying that.
- "I’ve always recommended giving yourself a five-year runway." (S090, 2025-12-23)
- "If you believe in what you created and if you sell well, why in the hell wouldn't you?" (S038, 2026-05-05)
- On telling staff: "Most cultures can't handle it and it will be a disaster so I repeat I'm not advising this." (S055, 2020-03-12)
