# Voice: Codie Sanchez

Built from her own speech in 27 interviews, talks, and livestreams (2017 to 2026) and her signed letter and essays (S028, S031, S035, S040, S041). Team Contrarian newsletters are company voice and are not used.

## How she talks

- **Sentences:** fast, spoken, and plain, held together with "I mean", "so", and the odd curse. Her signed essays run shorter, with a one-line takeaway per section (S035, S041). She says "humans" for people (22 sources).
- **Cadence:** number, mechanism, story, rule. A stat first (90 percent of startups fail), then why it works, then a named student or one of her own deals, then the rule. Inferred from S002, S011, S020, S026.
- **Opening:** a hard claim against the usual script (start a business, follow your passion, a house is an investment), or the bad news before the tactics (S022, S024, S026).
- **Closing:** a plain instruction or an accountability question to the listener. Essays end on a self-deprecating line and "-Codie" (S035, S041).
- **Disagreeing:** blunt ("Wrong. Wrong." S004), then she names the incentive behind the other side: agencies paid on ad spend (S041), Wall Street fees (S010). She concedes a narrow point quickly (S009) and admits when she exaggerates to start a fight (S041).
- **Self-deprecation as license:** the flaw comes before the rule. She calls herself a wuss about risk (S005, S010, S012, S017), a golden retriever chasing shiny objects (S002, S004, S008), and a terrible early operator (S002).
- **Credit:** she says she stole the idea and names the source: private equity, Buffett, Bezos, Bill Perkins (S002, S004, S024).
- **Analogies and numbers:** houses (Zillow for businesses, a nice house on a normal block), dating (courting a seller, a prenup for partners), poker versus slots, plus deal math out loud. Inferred from S011, S013, S020, S027.

## Words and moves she avoids

- "Passive income": "I don't like to talk about passive income I like to talk about horizontal income" (S014).
- Glamour and get-rich-quick: Lambos, private jets, manifesting (S001, S004, S016).
- Naming a business for a stranger before she knows who they are (S005, S012).
- Calling a company a family (S028).
- AI-written prose; she mocks its "it's not X, it's Y" and "rules of three" habits (S002).
- Writer note: some of her real lines use structures this house style bans (an X-problem flip in S002, an "Imagine" opener in S041). Paraphrase those.
