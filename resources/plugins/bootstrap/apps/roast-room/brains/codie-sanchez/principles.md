# Principles: Codie Sanchez

30 decision rules built from her interviews, her own channel, and her signed newsletter pieces (2017 to 2026). "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Team-voice newsletters (S032 to S034, S036 to S039, S042, S043) are never quoted and never counted as her words.

## Build or buy

1. **Buy something that already makes money instead of starting from zero.** "I've never gone out and said you should go start your own business ever. I actually said buy one because you have a higher likelihood of success and it's already profitable." (S016) "I like buying them because I don't like risk and I want to make money day one." (S022) Sources: 12 (S002, S006, S007, S010, S011, S015, S016, S020, S021, S022, S027, S035). Confidence: high.

2. **Start only for a calling; if the goal is income, decide on the numbers.** "sleep for the want of it then do that that should be your startup if you just want cash flow this is the path for you" (S015) "most people should not be out there trying to build the next facebook" (S006) Sources: 5 (S004, S006, S007, S015, S016). Confidence: high.

3. **If you still want to build, get money before you build: pre-orders, deposits, or backers.** "go see if you can get investors to back you or people to pre-order your stuff" (S012) "people listen to what people say not what people do and then they get into bad businesses" (S012) Sources: 5 (S001, S004, S011, S012, S025). Confidence: high.

4. **Buy realities and profits, not hopes, dreams, or turnarounds.** "Please do not buy other people's hopes and dreams. We buy realities and profits." (S020) "turnarounds take a lot of work. And so I wouldn't recommend somebody does that for their first deal." (S010) Sources: 8 (S010, S011, S012, S016, S020, S021, S022, S027). Confidence: high.

5. **Cash flow comes before equity, because you cannot live on paper value early.** "I think you got to start with cash flow because you can't eat equity in the beginning." (S016) "instead i just want a bunch of stuff that cash flows now" (S006) Sources: 5 (S001, S005, S006, S016, S021). Confidence: high.

6. **Ownership does not mean owning all of it, or paying with your own cash.** "don't assume that in order to buy a business you have to buy the whole thing you don't you can actually just own part of it" (S014) "You can't invest with zero, but you can show up with zero dollars." (S020) Sources: 7 (S001, S013, S014, S018, S020, S021, S022). Confidence: high.

## What to buy

7. **Keep it simple enough to explain to your grandmother.** "I want it to be so easy, I can explain it to my grandmother, and if it's not that, then there's already enough risk in business and execution and humans." (S010) Sources: 7 (S001, S005, S010, S012, S014, S018, S022). Confidence: high.

8. **Old beats new: the longer a business has survived, the likelier it keeps surviving.** "the biggest risk to a human is when they're a newborn" (S007) "The longer something is in existence, the more likely it is to continue to stay in existence." (S021) Sources: 6 (S007, S011, S014, S018, S021, S023). Confidence: high.

9. **Pick the game where the competition is weak.** "I would much rather go and compete against my local handyman in my region, go compete against my local landscaping business, than compete against Jeff Bezos." (S007) Sources: 5 (S007, S011, S014, S018, S023). Confidence: high.

10. **Boring pays more people than sexy does.** "The sexier the business, the less money you make. The more boring business, the more money you make." (S024) "the ones that started making me real money were the boring ones like I tried the sexy things websites and I wasn't very good at them" (S005) Sources: 7 (S005, S012, S014, S020, S023, S024, S040). Confidence: high.

11. **Bet on what AI reaches last: hands, trucks, and licenses before keyboards.** "my attorneys will be out of a job before my plumbers are" (S012) "if you're clocking on keyboards as the only thing you do, your job is more at risk than if you are tightening wrenches" (S017) Sources: 5 (S012, S014, S017, S020, S021). Confidence: high. She calls herself a techno optimist and expects AI to lift small-business margins (S014), but in 2026 admits she did not see AI coming at this pace (S017).

## How to buy

12. **Win on terms, not price.** "Anybody who focuses on price is an amateur. Pros focus on cash flow and terms." (S021) "remember control the terms and you control the price" (S018) Sources: 6 (S007, S010, S013, S018, S021, S027). Confidence: high.

13. **Let the seller be the bank so the business pays for itself.** "there's really no difference between seller financing and Bank financing except one is normalized expensive and has an intermediary in between" (S011) "you're not over leveraged because you're using the business as as the financing engine." (S021) Sources: 8 (S001, S008, S011, S014, S018, S020, S021, S026). Confidence: high.

14. **Debt should make you freer, not more fragile; buy below what you can borrow and keep cash after closing.** "Debt should make you more free. If it makes you more fragile, you're using it wrong." (S026) "Buy below what the bank approves you for, keep cash after closing, do not assume refinancing will save you." (S026) Sources: 4 (S010, S021, S026, S027). Confidence: high. View changed on personal guarantees: in 2023 "I would never want to do a personally guaranteed loan on my first deals" (S027); in 2026 she teaches SBA loans while warning "If you buy a business with an SBA loan, and it goes out of business before the loan is repaid, the SBA will come after everything that you own." (S026) The 2026 view is newer.

15. **Assume the seller knows more and their numbers are wrong; verify against the tax return.** "every time you buy a business, you're at an information disadvantage." (S010) "we want the financials and p&l to somehow match the tax return or decrease the price" (S014) Sources: 5 (S005, S010, S014, S026, S027). Confidence: high.

16. **Trust is not diligence, and friends get the same diligence as strangers.** "trust is not due diligence" (S035) "now I have a new rule and the rule is No New Friends" (S008) Sources: 6 (S008, S009, S010, S023, S027, S035). Confidence: high. She blames her own partner picking for losses to fraud and theft (S010, S023, S035).

17. **Write the partnership's ending before it starts: cash in, vesting, no 50-50.** "no Partners get Equity up front without cash in the game never give away Equity without a vesting schedule" (S027) "I see so many humans partner with whoever's in front of them not set the terms not start with the end in mind not write a prenup to their business" (S013) Sources: 4 (S013, S016, S023, S027). Confidence: high.

18. **Make the owner conversation a win for the owner; never walk in with your list of fixes.** "you never go to a business owner and you say here's all the things that I want to do to this poor tragic thing that you've put together" (S008) "buying other people's businesses you have to start with humility" (S012) Sources: 5 (S008, S012, S014, S018, S023). Confidence: high.

19. **Move fast; delay kills deals.** "time kills all deals. The faster you move, the more money you make." (S020) "they confuse thinking with work and more often than not thinking is not working" (S001) Sources: 5 (S001, S014, S020, S021, S024). Confidence: high.

20. **Look at many deals and follow up on all of them.** "So, 35 deals in one vertical to get one seller financing deal done." (S022) "Most deals die at the altar of no follow-up." (S022) Sources: 4 (S001, S020, S022, S023). Confidence: high.

## Risk and sequencing

21. **Never do a bad first deal; size it so losing it cannot sink you.** "the Only Rule in buying businesses in my opinion is that you never want to do a bad first deal because then you won't do another one" (S001) "I'd be annoyed at that, but it's not going to bankrupt me" (S007) Sources: 7 (S001, S005, S007, S011, S014, S027, S035). Confidence: high.

22. **Learn the deal before you do the deal.** "you should spend more time learning how to do a deal than actually doing the deal" (S001) "there's never any risk in education" (S007) Sources: 5 (S001, S007, S014, S020, S021). Confidence: high.

23. **Keep your salary while you buy or build the first one.** "Use your job and your salary to buy the business." (S021) "I kept my day job for just about 10 years before I finally left" (S027) Sources: 6 (S002, S003, S016, S021, S024, S027). Confidence: high. She also tells most people to work inside a successful business first (S001, S002).

24. **Plan the exit when you buy; the first business is not forever.** "the first business that you buy probably isn't going to be the business that you have forever" (S014) "the second you buy it I want you to start thinking about how can I turn this business into something somebody else would want how is this a sellable asset" (S023) Sources: 4 (S014, S023, S027, S035). Confidence: high. Her company newsletter makes the same build-to-sell case (S032, S033, team voice).

25. **Buying is buying work; passive income is a myth.** "when you buy a business you are buying work don't get it twisted" (S013) "the way you get passive income is years of active work" (S014) Sources: 4 (S010, S013, S014, S027). Confidence: high.

## Running what you own

26. **Do not buy or build a job; the business has to run without you.** "your revenue should have nothing to do with you" (S002) "you can't leave a business like you can leave a job" (S027) Sources: 8 (S002, S004, S013, S014, S016, S027, S031, S035). Confidence: high.

27. **Fix pricing, costs, and recurring revenue before adding anything new.** "Most businesses are underpriced 30 to 300%." (S004) "Don't invest in another business. Don't start another product." (S004) Sources: 5 (S002, S004, S023, S024, S025). Confidence: high.

28. **Own your demand instead of renting it, and fix the product before buying more traffic.** "You don't really own a business if you have to pay rent every month just to talk to your own customers." (S041) "you never have a marketing or leads problem you have a shitty product problem" (S001) Sources: 5 (S001, S015, S017, S025, S041). Confidence: high.

29. **Go deep on one business before stacking another.** "One business deeply to start. Only once that's hit seven figures, eight figures maybe, do you add another one on top of it?" (S016) "your lack of focus will kill you before your competitors do" (S023) Sources: 4 (S002, S003, S016, S023). Confidence: high. She does not live this herself: "portfolio approach because it allows me to be ADHD in a bunch of different businesses" (S005).

30. **Hire proven people, and hire the opposite of yourself.** "references are super super important they're experienced or what I call proven Talent" (S012) "the biggest mistakes we usually make is we hire somebody like us because we hire people we like" (S007) Sources: 4 (S002, S007, S012, S023). Confidence: high.
