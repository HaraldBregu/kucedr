# Dan Martell: principles

Decision rules he applies again and again. Confidence: high = 3 or more supporting sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Web posts on danmartell.com (S110 to S128) carry no date in the corpus and are marked "post."

## Founder time

1. **Hire to buy back time, not to add capacity.** "Most entrepreneurs should not hire to add capacity to their business. They should hire to buyback time out of their calendar." [S060, 2022] Sources: 20 (see frameworks, Buyback Principle). Confidence: high.

2. **The business grows only as fast as the founder delegates.** "Your business is capped at the speed of your delegation." [S036, 2025] "Your capacity doubles the moment you stop making every decision." [S036, 2025] Sources: S036, S122, S123, S113. Confidence: high.

3. **Low-value work cannot build a big company.** "Million dollar companies were not built off ten dollar tasks" [S019, 2023] "Nobody's ever built a million dollar business doing $10 task. It's impossible." [S011, 2025] Sources: S011, S017, S019, S031, S037, S038, S109. Confidence: high.

4. **Spend money to save time.** "Broke people spend time to save money rich people spend money to save time." [S038, 2024] Sources: S007, S023, S030, S033, S038, S122. Confidence: high.

5. **If you can't afford help, the problem is your pricing or your value.** "If you can't afford it means you're not charging enough you haven't become valuable enough you're not willing to sacrifice" [S019, 2023] Sources: S019, S020, S038. Confidence: high.

6. **The founder without an assistant is an expensive assistant.** "If you don't have an executive assistant you actually do have one it's you and you're an incredibly expensive one." [S020, 2023] "Your inbox is nothing more than a public to-do list for strangers goals and dreams on the internet." [S020, 2023] Sources: S013, S014, S020, S031, S038, S049, S054, S097 (inbox line in 9 sources). Confidence: high.

7. **Plan the freed hours before you buy them.** "most people hire folks before they know what they're going to do with that new time so then they feel lazy then they feel guilty." [S108, 2024] "I'm not a fan of people just like buying back their time and hanging out on the beach." [S109, 2025] Sources: S014, S017, S020, S031, S052, S061, S108, S109. Confidence: high.

8. **Calendars and bank accounts reveal real priorities.** "I can tell your priorities by two things. Your bank account and your calendar." [S017, 2026] Sources: S001, S007, S013, S015, S017, S033, S039. Confidence: high.

9. **Manage energy, not time.** "You can't manage time you can only manage the energy that you've got within that time." [S050, 2023] "Time is never the constraint. ENERGY is." [S110, post] Sources: S025, S026, S033, S039, S041, S050, S052, S110. Confidence: high.

10. **Your best skill becomes your scaling bottleneck, so hand it off too.** "Your number one strength as a CEO becomes your Achilles heel to scaling." [S060, 2022] Sources: S030, S060, S109. Confidence: high. Counterweight: keep the work that is high value and lights you up (S043, S076).

## Hiring

11. **Hire character, train skill.** "Hire for the soul, train for the role." [S036, 2025] "What you can't hire or pay for is character and drive." [S074, 2021] Sources: S013, S035, S036, S040, S074, S094, S109, S122. Confidence: high.

12. **See the work before you hire.** "I never hire somebody until I have the opportunity to collaborate with them" [S030, 2024] Sources: 14 (see frameworks, Test-First Hiring). Confidence: high.

13. **Hire from alignment; an empty seat beats a bad hire.** "I don't hire from a place of desperation. I hire from a place of alignment." [S013, 2025] "a lot more disruptive to a team to have somebody come and leave than to just never fill the spot for a few months." [S013, 2025] Sources: S013, S059, S074. Confidence: high.

14. **Hire people who tell you what to do.** "When I hire somebody, I don't tell them what to do. I have them tell me what to do." [S059, 2022] "I didn't hire you to tell you how to do your job" [S031, 2024] Sources: S031, S059, S070. Confidence: high.

15. **For support roles, hire your opposite.** "Don't hire somebody that maps to you you want to find the opposite." [S108, 2024] Sources: S014, S049, S055, S108. Confidence: high.

16. **Senior hires must bring playbooks; pay professionals.** "If you think that it's cheaper to hire an amateur you should see what happens when you hire a professional." [S053, 2023] "Do they come with playbooks, do they come with process?" [S031, 2024] Sources: S031, S053, S109, S123. Confidence: high. Corollary: never pay with an inflated title (S053).

## Leading the people you hired

17. **Delegate outcomes, not tasks.** "Delegate the outcome, not the task." [S031, 2024] Sources: S014, S031, S036, S061, S113, S121, S122. Confidence: high.

18. **Train, don't tell; most performance gaps are training gaps.** "if you train them they'll scale if you tell them you'll fail" [S030, 2024] "The gap between what you expect and what your team delivers is almost always a training gap, not a talent one." [S113, post] Sources: S017, S030, S058, S108, S113, S115, S121. Confidence: high.

19. **Fix the process, then train, then judge the person.** "fix the process train the person if it happens again now you know you have a people problem" [S030, 2024] Sources: S030, S085, S113, S115. Confidence: high.

20. **Accept 80% from others.** "80% done by somebody else is 100% freaking awesome." [S015, 2023] Sources: S007, S011, S013, S015, S020, S108, S123, S128. Confidence: high.

21. **Give access first, then verify.** "Access first. Accountability second." [S113, post] "it's delegate and verify" [S095, 2016] Sources: S009, S060, S095, S103, S113, S121. Confidence: high.

22. **One owner and one number for everything.** "When you have two people accountable for something you have nobody accountable for it." [S090, 2018] "Every person in all my companies have a number." [S031, 2024] Sources: S031, S075, S087, S088, S090, S121. Confidence: high.

23. **Standards and values are what you tolerate; hire and fire against them.** "Your standards are what you accept, not what you say they are." [S016, 2025] "To me, we hire and fire against values." [S083, 2019] Sources: S016, S036, S083, S085, S101, S115, S122, S123. Confidence: high.

24. **Trust your gut on people.** "The most money I've ever lost was in not listening to my gut. Period. Full stop." [S011, 2025] Sources: S011, S101, S022 (red flags on an acquisition). Confidence: high.

25. **Build people; give them a vision big enough for their dreams.** "We build the people, the people build the business." [S031, 2024] "Your dreams need to be so big that everybody else's dreams fit inside of them." [S058, 2022] Sources: S005, S028, S030, S031, S038, S058, S075, S108, S115, S117. Confidence: high.

26. **No business grows past its leader.** "No business will grow past the growth of the leader." [S058, 2022] Sources: S032, S038, S058, S061. Confidence: high.

28. **Pre-sell before you build.** "Pre-sell before you build, always. The biggest risk to your life is building something that nobody wants." [S035, 2026] Sources: S021, S030, S034, S035, S048, S065. Confidence: high.

29. **Most founders undercharge.** "Most people do not charge enough for their software or their products in their business or services. Period. Full stop." [S056, 2023] Sources: S024, S052, S056, S068, S089. Confidence: high.

30. **Retention before acquisition.** "Revenue is vanity. Retention is sanity." [S036, 2025] Sources: S036, S114, S122, S124. Confidence: high.
