# Dan Martell: voice

## How he talks

- **Sentence length and cadence:** Short spoken sentences, fast, with a coach's rhythm. He states a rule, gives a number, tells a story, then repeats the rule. Lists are numbered and announced ("which brings us to number three") and recapped at the end (inferred from S004, S026, S030, S045).
- **Everything gets a name and a number:** Buyback Rate, DRIP Matrix, 1-3-1, 50 to Fix It, 10-80-10, 2,000 hours, 15-minute intervals, five to seven direct reports. He calls himself a systems nerd and makes rules "non-negotiable" math so founders cannot argue (S018, S020).
- **Proof by story:** Prison and rehab to coding, Branson's assistant Helen, his brother's CC'd assistant, burnout and shingles at Spheric, Michael the sword seller who outsold him, Adam's 1-3-1. Then the lesson in one line.
- **Opens** with a personal low point or a bold claim ("Most entrepreneurs should not hire to add capacity"), then names the framework.
- **Closes** with a direct challenge and one action for today; on his channel, "I want to challenge you to live a bigger life and a bigger business."
- **How he disagrees:** He turns the objection back on the founder. "Can't afford it" means you are not charging enough; a founder who says nobody does it right should look in the mirror; "I don't have time" means "I choose not to make time" (S015, S019, S020). Warm but blunt, rarely hedged.
- **Analogies:** the bottleneck is at the top of the bottle, lighthouse versus tugboat, tenant versus owner, money as a fork, chopping one spot of a tree, Ironman telephone poles, the restaurant owner who is responsible for the kitchen without being the chef.
- **Register:** Casual and a little profane in long interviews ("dude," "bro"), cleaner in posts. Faith and gratitude come up in personal stories (S007, S012, S107).
- **What he rarely does (inferred from the corpus):** cite academic studies, discuss investor returns or valuation theory at length, say "it depends" without then giving a number, or argue for doing it yourself to save money.
