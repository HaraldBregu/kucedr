## Offer

**1. The offer is the biggest lever. Make it so good that saying no feels stupid, and fix it before pushing harder on sales or copy.**
- "nailing the offer is the single strongest lever on your business success" (S114)
- "If you have a good enough offer, you don't have to sell hard. You don't have to have amazing copy or amazing creative." (S117)
- Sources: 9 (S012, S013, S043, S045, S099, S106, S114, S117, S118). Confidence: high.

**2. Win on speed and ease, the bottom of the value equation, not on bigger promises.**
- "Speed is one of the best all-time competitive advantages." (S115)
- "If you remove everything that sucks about getting what someone wants, by default, you will have something good." (S022)
- Sources: 11 (S003, S016, S022, S050, S051, S070, S078, S092, S094, S115, S118). Confidence: high.

**3. Decommoditize. Be different enough that buyers cannot compare you on price.**
- "When you sell in a vacuum you no longer have to compete on price." (S023)
- "85% of them competed on price and only 1% of them won on price" (S060)
- Sources: 9 (S020, S021, S023, S027, S046, S060, S101, S107, S114). Confidence: high.

**4. Go narrow on who and what problem. Narrow raises price and lowers CAC; you earn the right to go broad.**
- "the more specific you get the more you can charge" (S054)
- "You need to earn the right to go broad." (S012)
- Sources: 8 (S012, S024, S032, S044, S052, S054, S094, S121). Confidence: high.

**5. The market beats the operator. Pick a market in pain, with money, easy to reach, and growing.**
- "great entrepreneur, poor market, market wins." (S008)
- "We have to understand that the market is always going to be the strongest variable in how much money you make." (S002)
- Sources: 9 (S002, S008, S046, S047, S067, S107, S113, S114, S121). Confidence: high.

## Price

**6. Price is the biggest profit lever. Charge more and add value rather than serve more people for less.**
- "Optimizing pricing has a six times stronger increase on profitability than getting more customers" (S015)
- "If you raise prices, you make more doing the same or less work." (S113)
- Sources: 11 (S005, S015, S019, S021, S022, S034, S060, S106, S113, S114, S123). Confidence: high.

**7. Sell to people with money and avoid the middle.**
- "Either sell extremely expensive stuff to a select few or sell something super cheap to everyone. The middle is where people die." (S050)
- "the value derive is often about who you find not necessarily what you provide" (S114)
- Sources: 11 (S003, S021, S043, S050, S067, S090, S091, S101, S111, S113, S114). Confidence: high.

**8. Never discount and never negotiate price. Change the terms, components, or asks instead.**
- "I never discount but I will change terms." (S046)
- "I'm a very firm believer in never negotiating with terrorists, including price terrorists." (S051)
- Sources: 9 (S011, S026, S033, S046, S051, S074, S101, S113, S123). Confidence: high.

**9. Settle pricing and offer questions with math or tests, not opinions, and optimize for total money, not the most yeses.**
- "there are two types of questions that you shouldn't ask questions that that can be solved with a spreadsheet and questions that can be solved with testing" (S042)
- "The sweet spot isn't the most yes's, it's the most money." (S021)
- Sources: 7 (S013, S015, S021, S030, S042, S049, S115). Confidence: high.

**10. When demand exceeds supply, cut supply and raise price. Do not market into a supply constraint.**
- "When you have demand, cut supply." (S050)
- "More marketing on a supply constrained does nothing." (S092)
- Sources: 10 (S012, S021, S022, S027, S028, S032, S050, S066, S092, S108). Confidence: high.

## Money model and unit economics

**11. Run the business on lifetime gross profit to CAC, not on the cheapest lead.**
- "What we are optimizing around is for return, not for cost per lead." (S012)
- Sources: 10 (S012, S013, S014, S019, S025, S042, S061, S089, S100, S113). Confidence: high.

**12. The model beats the method. Leads rarely cost too much; you make too little per customer.**
- "Leads costing too much is rarely ever an issue the issue is usually that you don't make enough money per customer." (S023)
- "fundamentally, the business that can make more money from its customer than its competitors wins." (S089)
- Sources: 8 (S003, S013, S020, S023, S060, S084, S089, S113). Confidence: high.

**13. Make more gross profit in the first 30 days than the customer cost to get and serve, then recycle the cash.**

- "I'm using my customers money to finance all of the growth in this business." (S037)
- Sources: 9 (S020, S037, S045, S046, S060, S070, S078, S089, S113). Confidence: high.

**14. Cash flow is survival. Get paid sooner and up front.**

- "if you want to make more money ask to get paid sooner" (S060)
- Sources: 11 (S015, S020, S026, S028, S039, S046, S060, S061, S089, S113, S122). Confidence: high.

**15. Make the next offer at the point of greatest deprivation, right after the first yes.**

- "It's easier to get the second yes after you get the first yes." (S046)
- Sources: 9 (S005, S017, S020, S030, S045, S046, S074, S098, S116). Confidence: high.
