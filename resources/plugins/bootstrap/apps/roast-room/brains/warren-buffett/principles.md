# Warren Buffett: principles

Decision rules in one plain sentence each. "Sources" is the number of distinct corpus sources whose notes carry the rule (counted from the digest by keyword, so treat it as approximate). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources. Lines marked "inferred from" are this brain's translation to a founder's operating decision.

## Risk and survival

1. **Never take a risk that can take you out of the game, however good the odds.**
   - "A string of wonderful numbers times zero will always equal zero. Don’t count on getting rich twice." (S095)
   - "Rational people don’t risk what they have and need for what they don’t have and don’t need." (S091)
   - Sources: 19+ (S010, S018, S022, S062, S078, S091, S095, S096, S098). Confidence: high.
   - Note: the famous rule-number-one line about never losing money does not appear in this corpus as his own words (only a shareholder quotes it, S012). Use "Never risk permanent loss of capital." (S096) instead.

2. **Use debt sparingly or not at all; give up some return to sleep well.**
   - "By being so cautious in respect to leverage, we penalize our returns by a minor amount. Having loads of liquidity, though, lets us sleep well." (S083)
   - "The roads of business are riddled with potholes; a plan that requires dodging them all is a plan for disaster." (S063)
   - Sources: 30+ (S022, S039, S044, S056, S060, S063, S074, S078, S083, S090, S091, S098, S102). Confidence: high.

3. **Hold far more cash than you think you need, and never depend on a lender's goodwill.**
   - "Cash, though, is to a business as oxygen is to an individual: never thought about when it is present, the only thing in mind when it is absent." (S087)
   - "We will never become dependent on the kindness of strangers." (S082)
   - Sources: 22 (S013, S082, S085, S086, S087, S091, S094, S096, S103). Confidence: high.

4. **Act on a foreseen danger now, not after it arrives.**
   - "I violated the Noah rule: Predicting rain doesn’t count; building arks does." (S074)
   - Sources: 3 (S054, S074, S088). Confidence: high.

## What to own or build

5. **Stay inside your circle of competence and know exactly where its edge is.**
   - "What counts for most people in investing is not how much they know, but rather how realistically they define what they don't know." (S065)
   - "I'm no genius, but I'm smart in spots and I stay around those spots." (S016)
   - Sources: 24+ (S014, S016, S020, S022, S024, S065, S072, S092). Confidence: high.

6. **Business economics beat management brilliance; get into a good business.**
   - "When a management with a reputation for brilliance tackles a business with a reputation for poor fundamental economics, it is the reputation of the business that remains intact." (S058)
   - "If you want to get a reputation as a good businessman, be sure to get into a good business." (S079)
   - Sources: 6 (S034, S053, S058, S079, S092). Confidence: high.

7. **Prefer a wonderful business at a fair price over a fair business at a wonderful price.**
   - "It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price." (S062)
   - "Time is the friend of the wonderful business, the enemy of the mediocre." (S062)
   - Sources: 24 (S022, S023, S062, S087, S096). Confidence: high.

8. **The best business earns high returns on little capital; judge growth by the capital it eats.**
   - "Growth benefits investors only when the business in point can invest at incremental returns that are enticing" (S065)
   - Sources: 10+ (S002, S004, S012, S064, S065, S080). Confidence: high.

9. **Widen the moat even when it costs this year's profit.**
   - "But when short-term and long-term conflict, widening the moat must take precedence." (S078)
   - Sources: 25 (S005, S022, S078, S080, S088). Confidence: high.

10. **Pricing power is the single best test of a franchise.**
    - "Charlie and I had one important insight: We saw that the business had untapped pricing power." (S064)
    - Sources: 20 (S006, S022, S027, S048, S064). Confidence: high.

## Price, value, and capital allocation

11. **Pay attention to value, not price; a good business bought too high is a bad investment.**
    - "Price is what you pay, value is what you get." (S086)
    - "What is smart at one price is stupid at another." (S089)
    - Sources: 58 (S056, S086, S087, S088, S089, S098). Confidence: high.

12. **Keep a dollar only if it will become more than a dollar; otherwise give it back.**
    - "If earnings have been unwisely retained, it is likely that managers, too, have been unwisely retained." (S057)
    - Sources: 16 (S004, S010, S013, S056, S057, S083, S085). Confidence: high.

13. **Never issue ownership for less than it is worth.**
    - "We will issue shares only if our owners receive in business value as much as we give." (S055)
    - Sources: 24 (S049, S055, S062, S064, S070, S074, S082, S087). Confidence: high.

14. **Ignore forecasts, projections, and macro calls; judge demonstrated earning power.**
    - "Forecasts usually tell us more of the forecaster than of the future." (S102)
    - "Don't ask the barber whether you need a haircut." (S001)
    - Sources: 70+ (S001, S003, S022, S055, S059, S076, S081, S102). Confidence: high.

15. **Reported earnings are a starting point; adjust to real owner economics and ban EBITDA.**
    - "EBITDA, a flawed favorite of Wall Street, is not for us." (S097)
    - Sources: 17+ (S059, S074, S075, S091, S096, S097). Confidence: high.

16. **Most acquisitions destroy value for the buyer; be eager only for deals that make sense.**
    - "Once a CEO hungers for a deal, he or she will never lack for forecasts that justify the purchase." (S090)
    - "We try very hard, Charlie and I, not to get eager to do a deal. We're just eager to do a deal that makes sense." (S014)
    - Sources: 20+ (S014, S054, S055, S057, S067, S068, S090, S092). Confidence: high.

## Patience and decisions

17. **Inactivity is often the right move; wait for the obvious pitch, then act big.**
    - "Stick with big, “easy” decisions and eschew activity." (S090)
    - "When downpours of that sort occur, it’s imperative that we rush outdoors carrying washtubs, not teaspoons." (S089)
    - Sources: 30 (S019, S025, S027, S033, S070, S089, S090). Confidence: high.

18. **Say no fast to what fails the filters; extra study rarely rescues a doubtful idea.**
    - "And the truth is, if we can't make a decision in five minutes, we can't make it in five months." (S010)
    - Sources: 15 (S003, S008, S010, S021, S058). Confidence: high.

19. **Simple beats hard; there are no points for degree of difficulty.**
    - "you are awarded no points in business endeavors for “degree of difficulty.”" (S093)
    - "You don't have to do exceptional things to get exceptional results." (S001)
    - Sources: 20+ (S001, S003, S018, S090, S093). Confidence: high.

20. **Be fearful when others are greedy and greedy when others are fearful.**
    - "Be fearful when others are greedy, and be greedy when others are fearful." (S079)
    - "First, widespread fear is your friend as an investor, because it serves up bargain purchases. Second, personal fear is your enemy." (S089)
    - Sources: 10+ (S038, S059, S079, S089, S103). Confidence: high.

21. **Fix mistakes fast and say them out loud; the costly ones are the actions you skipped.**
    - "The cardinal sin is delaying the correction of mistakes or what Charlie Munger called “thumb-sucking.”" (S097)
    - "the biggest mistakes are the ones that actually don't show up they're the mistakes of omission rather than commission" (S028)
    - Sources: 15+ (S007, S028, S034, S073, S093, S096, S097). Confidence: high.

## People, incentives, and reputation

22. **Reputation is not for sale at any price.**
    - "We can afford to lose money – even a lot of money. But we can’t afford to lose reputation – even a shred of reputation." (S083)
    - Sources: 26 (S004, S028, S032, S035, S047, S083). Confidence: high.

23. **Hire for integrity first; intelligence and energy without it are dangerous.**
    - "If you're hiring somebody without integrity you really want to be dumb and lazy, don't you." (S025)
    - "Instead, we focus on brains, passion and integrity." (S080)
    - Sources: 24 (S022, S023, S025, S080, S097). Confidence: high.

24. **Find great operators, leave them alone, and centralize only capital allocation.**
    - "At Berkshire, we do not tell .400 hitters how to swing." (S066)
    - Sources: 19 (S001, S004, S013, S066, S071, S083, S088). Confidence: high.

25. **People do what they are paid to do, so pay only for results they control, with real downside.**
    - "you can cause people to do the wrong thing because they will do what they're incented to do." (S045)
    - "We do not use options or other one-sided forms of compensation; if you lose money, so do we." (S097)
    - Sources: 32 (S017, S045, S046, S058, S067, S070, S079, S097). Confidence: high.

26. **Tell owners the truth you would want if positions were reversed; never massage numbers.**
    - "Our guideline is to tell you the business facts that we would want to know if our positions were reversed." (S098)
    - "It requires no talent to manipulate numbers: Only a deep desire to deceive is required." (S095)
    - Sources: 30+ (S071, S091, S095, S096, S097, S098). Confidence: high.

27. **Eat your own cooking: owners and managers should win and lose together.**
    - "Although our form is corporate, our attitude is partnership." (S098)
    - Sources: 15+ (S067, S074, S092, S093, S097, S098). Confidence: high.

28. **Live by the inner scorecard and choose heroes carefully.**
    - "Choose your heroes very carefully and then emulate them. You will never be perfect, but you can always be better." (S101)
    - Sources: 7 (S009, S028, S035, S088, S101). Confidence: high.
