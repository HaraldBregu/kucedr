# Principles: Leila Hormozi

Built from the 48 videos in sources.md (2021 to 2026), weighted to how she decides about leaders, teams, and scaling. 30 decision rules. Sources is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **The company is capped by its leader, so the first person who has to change is the founder.** "you cannot have a billion dollar business without being a billion dollar ceo you can't have a million dollar business without being a million dollar ceo" (S041) "if you want the business to grow then you have to grow if you want the business to change then you have to change and so it all starts at the top" (S033) Sources: 9 (S002, S006, S021, S023, S026, S027, S033, S041, S047). Confidence: high.

2. **Getting the right people in the right seats is the CEO's most important job.** "the most important job that you can have as CEO is make sure you have the right people in the right seats." (S015) "your number one job as a leader is to find the people to help you build your business" (S037) Sources: 5 (S001, S005, S015, S023, S037). Confidence: high.

3. **Hire for character and values, then train the skill.** "we don't hire people who know how to make beds we hire people that are good people" (S012) "I would rather take somebody who has less of the skill that I need and train them up to the best of my ability" (S033) Sources: 7 (S008, S011, S012, S021, S033, S039, S048). Confidence: high.

4. **Bet on the jockey, and run no race rather than the wrong jockey.** "a great leader a great CEO can make an average idea succeed and a poor leader can make even the best idea fail" (S026) "if I can't find a jockey that I like no jockey is better than the wrong jockey" (S026) Sources: 4 (S007, S012, S026, S041). Confidence: high.

5. **Do not hire into inconsistency or panic; fix lead flow and sales first, and use contractors for short-term spikes.** "Like you need to get consistent lead flow and sales locked in before you hire a bunch of people." (S019) "I stopped hiring people out of desperation I stopped hiring people out of panic" (S024) Sources: 4 (S019, S024, S030, S032). Confidence: high.

6. **The founder stays in hiring until a proven executive team can carry it.** "i don't think that they can get above 20 or 30 people because they're not involving themselves in that" (S048) "the CEO would show with the most valuable resource they have, which is time, not money, time, how important those people are to them." (S003) In 2025 she brought Alex into select interviews while recruiting heavily (S023), so the rule bends with load but the founder stays on key hires. Sources: 5 (S003, S023, S037, S039, S048). Confidence: high.

7. **Speed wins candidates; the best ones are gone in about a week.** "if you're waiting more than eight days to hire somebody, you're getting worse candidate." (S003) The eight-day figure is her own number, unsourced. Sources: 2 (S003, S037). Confidence: medium.

8. **Trust evidence over gut and likability when judging people.** "People that are very likable usually get by by being likable, not by being competent." (S013) "your gut's not going to lead you to building the best team your gut's going to lead you to building a team that's just a bunch of replicas of who you already are" (S039) Sources: 6 (S001, S011, S013, S037, S039, S044). Confidence: high.

9. **Say what good looks like before you judge anyone.** "if you don't properly Define and verbalize your expectations then nobody knows how to win or lose the game they don't even know how to play the game" (S034) Sources: 6 (S020, S034, S038, S045, S046, S047). Confidence: high.

10. **Reinforce fast and keep punishment for ethics.** "the timing of your response to a behavior matters more than the power of the response" (S035) "the only time that I use negative reinforcement is when something is somebody does something immoral unethical or illegal" (S012) Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S012, S034, S035, S042). Confidence: high.

11. **Reward the attempt so people try again.** "I try really hard to reward people for trying even if their attempt was bad" (S010) "what you reward in your company is what gets repeated" (S028) Sources: 4 (S002, S010, S028, S033). Confidence: high.

12. **Thank people for bad news, because early bad news can still be fixed.** "Reward them when they tell you things that you don't want to hear." 13. **Withholding feedback is selfish, and every avoided conversation becomes dysfunction.** "I think that avoidance of giving feedback is selfish" (S028) "if you avoid the conversation you create long-term dysfunction" (S046) Sources: 4 (S020, S028, S046, S047). Confidence: high.

14. **Feedback only counts when behavior changes, and frequency beats duration.** "if you give feedback to somebody the behavior doesn't change the feedback was not feedback" (S011) "it's not about the time that you spend on a skill it's about the frequency at which you work on the skill" (S028) Sources: 5 (S003, S011, S028, S031, S032). Confidence: high.

15. **Before firing, do your part first, check seat, season, and boss, then judge the rate of improvement against what the business can bear.** "I always start with what I can do differently first and so even if that takes a little bit longer I'm okay with that" (S045) "if that person is not improving at a rate that your business can bear then you have to make the hard calls sometimes" (S031) Sources: 5 (S001, S015, S031, S043, S045). 16. **Keeping the wrong person sacrifices everyone else.** "I can't even tell you how many businesses have one person preventing the whole thing from growing" (S011) "i cannot risk the team and their payroll for everyone else's families to keep you here" (S043) Sources: 4 (S011, S022, S026, S043). 18. **Let people go fast, privately, generously, and without retaliation.** "I have found that when you let somebody go the more that you say the worse it is" (S011) "I will protect the company I will do whatever's in the best interest of the company I will not retaliate at them" (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S011, S030).
