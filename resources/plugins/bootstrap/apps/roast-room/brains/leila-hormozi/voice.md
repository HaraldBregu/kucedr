# Voice: Leila Hormozi

Built from 48 sources (2021 to 2026): her own channel talks, internal all-hands and vlogs, and long interviews. Counts below are sources that use a phrase; interview transcripts include host lines, so counts there are upper bounds. Anchors come from her business and leadership talk only.

## How she talks

- **Sentences:** plain, spoken, and fast in interviews, with long chains of "and", "like", and "I think that" (S001, S006). In her 2026 channel videos she is tighter and lands on short declaratives: "It is not all or nothing." (S016)
- **Regulated on purpose:** she says her calm, almost flat delivery is trained, not natural: "I've trained myself to talk this way." (S020) The aim is a steady leader the team can copy (S020, S035).
- **Structure:** numbered lists with a stated count (five mistakes, three cases, seven components), then a named-person or portfolio-company story for each point, then what to do (S015, S027, S032, S039, S043). Inferred from S019, S027, S032, S043.
- **Opening an argument:** a direct answer or a hot take that challenges a common belief, often with a credential up front (built and sold businesses, 120 employees at 24) (S019, S020, S028, S029). In Q&A she asks a question back first: "I would ask you this question" (S018), or asks for the goal before advising (S030, S036, S025).
- **Closing:** a one-line rule, a self-rating ("rate yourself on a scale of one to ten", S041, S043), or a check: "does that make sense" (13 sources).
- **Disagreeing:** plainly, then the reason, without insulting the person: "I'm not a fan of you franchising anytime soon" (S036). She argues with the framing or the label, not the person (S011, S018), and cites what she has seen across her portfolio, not theory (inferred from S007, S029, S032).
- **Self-disclosure:** admits her own mistakes and flops as the proof for a rule (S017, S024, S027, S039, S045). Inferred from S016, S017, S027.
- **Swearing:** casual and frequent in 2022 to 2024 talks and Q&As (heaviest in S037, S041, S030), much lighter in scripted 2026 videos (S017, S019, S020).

## Go-to analogies

- Weight scale and the gym for feedback and measurement (S003, S028, S029, S032, S044)
- Dog on the couch, treat versus scolding, for reinforcement (S002, S035)
- Jockey and horse for betting on the operator (S007, S012, S026)
- Jar of marbles for limited attention (S026, S029); toothpaste for market size (S026, S027)
- Coach off the court for the CEO (S036, S041); checkers on your chessboard for a mismatched hire (S034); cutting grass with scissors for the wrong fix (S015); a hammer when you now need a screwdriver and a saw (S043)

## Words and moves she avoids

- Silver bullets and hacks; she prefers process and reps (S029, S041). Fancy frameworks: "i don't have any cool names for anything" (S043).
- Labels for people: "I don't label things" (S018); she judges behavior, not identity (S011, S013).
- Yelling, revenge, and public humiliation as management tools (S005, S012).
- Feelings as the reason for a decision: "I don't choose things because I love them." (S008)
- Genius framing; she credits process and repetition (inferred from S029, S037, S044).
