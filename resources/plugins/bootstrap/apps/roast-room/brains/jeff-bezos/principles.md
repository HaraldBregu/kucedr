# Principles: Jeff Bezos

28 decision rules. Confidence: high = 3 or more distinct sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Source counts treat S002/S044 and S026/S030 as one.

1. **Center the company on customers, not competitors, because customers are never satisfied and keep pulling you forward even when you lead.**
   "Whereas customers are always unsatisfied. They're always discontent. They always want more." (S003) / "in my view, obsessive customer focus is by far the most protective of Day 1 vitality." (S064)
   Sources: 15+ (S003, S004, S009, S011, S014, S024, S030, S037, S038, S039, S044, S047, S060, S062, S064). Confidence: high.

2. **Invent what customers cannot ask for; listening and surveys are necessary but never enough.**
   "No customer ever asked Amazon to create the Prime membership program, but it sure turns out they wanted it." (S041) / "Market research doesn't help." (S066)
   Sources: 9 (S014, S021, S024, S025, S027, S041, S057, S064, S066). Confidence: high.

3. **Run on a five to seven year horizon; most people will not defer gratification, which is your edge.**
   "You can defer gratification and think long term, that will give you a head start against all of your competitors because most people can't do that." (S024)
   Sources: 8 (S003, S014, S024, S027, S038, S051, S056, S060). Confidence: high.

4. **Separate the stock from the company and manage the company.**
   "the stock is not the company and the company is not the stock." (S044) / "if the stock is up 10% this month don't feel 10% smarter because when the stock is down 10% some month you're going to have to feel 10% dumber" (S018)
   Sources: 7 (S003, S008, S018, S023, S044, S048, S060). Confidence: high.

5. **Speed is a cultural trait; keep the advantages of scale by deciding fast.**
   "Decisiveness is about speed, and speed matters in business." (S007) / "The way you stay nimble is by making decisions fast." (S025)
   Sources: 5 (S007, S025, S045, S064, S066). Confidence: high.

6. **Go slow on one-way doors and never skip steps; shortcuts only look faster.**
   "I'm the chief slowdown officer" (S025) / "Skipping steps slows you down." (S032)
   Sources: 6 (S007, S017, S025, S032, S035, S063). Confidence: high.

7. **Be stubborn on the vision and flexible on the details.**
   "On the details, we at Amazon are always flexible, but on matters of vision we are stubborn and relentless." (S068)
   Sources: 6 (S010, S011, S023, S036, S063, S068). Confidence: high.

8. **People who are right a lot change their minds a lot; hunt for evidence against your firmest beliefs.**
   "I've noticed that people who are right a lot change their mind a lot." (S024) / "I'm going to actively try to look for evidence to disconfirm that idea." (S036)
   Sources: 4 (S023, S024, S036, S045). Confidence: high.

9. **Anything new means failure and being misunderstood; accept both.**
   "If you're going to do anything new or innovative, you have to be willing to be misunderstood." (S003) / "Failure inevitably comes along with invention and risk-taking, which is why we try to make Amazon the best place in the world to fail." (S041)
   Sources: 9 (S003, S010, S014, S029, S037, S041, S047, S061, S063). Confidence: high.

10. **Take many bold bets, but never one that can sink the company.**
    "the big winners pay for thousands of failed experiments." (S044) / "I don't believe in bet the company bets that's when you're desperate" (S018)
    Sources: 6 (S018, S025, S036, S038, S044, S063). Confidence: high.

11. **When the data and the anecdotes disagree, suspect the metric; metrics and process are proxies.**
    "When the data and the anecdotes disagree, the anecdotes are usually right." (S045) / "The process is not the thing. It's always worth asking, do we own the process or does the process own us?" (S064)
    Sources: 3 (S003, S045, S064). Confidence: high.

12. **Use analysis whenever it can decide; the biggest calls come down to judgment, intuition, and taste.**
    "When you can make a decision with analysis, you should do so, but it turns out in life that your most important decisions are always made with instinct, intuition, taste, heart." (S044)
    Sources: 6 (S011, S023, S024, S044, S053, S064). Confidence: high.

13. **Fix the root cause so the fix works for every customer, not just the one who complained.**
    "you don't just fix the symptom you have to fix the root cause and that's been the secret to our operational success for 20 years" (S022)
    Sources: 7 (S006, S022, S029, S033, S044, S049, S050). Confidence: high.

14. **Price to earn trust; never raise prices just because you can.**
    "just because you could raise prices doesn't mean you should" (S009) / "Our pricing objective is to earn customer trust, not to optimize short-term profit dollars." (S056)
    Sources: 7 (S009, S037, S049, S050, S051, S053, S056). Confidence: high.

15. **Trust is earned slowly by making hard promises and keeping them.**
    "You earn trust slowly, over time, by doing hard things well" (S041) / "that's how you earn trust make a hard promise keep it" (S037)
    Sources: 4 (S037, S041, S047, S060). Confidence: high.

16. **Choose future cash flows over good-looking accounting.**
    "When forced to choose between optimizing the appearance of our GAAP accounting and maximizing the present value of future cash flows, we'll take the cash flows." (S038)
    Sources: 4 (S038, S050, S052, S056). Confidence: high.

17. **The hiring bar is the most important lever; every hire should raise the group's average.**
    "Setting the bar high in our approach to hiring has been, and will continue to be, the single most important element of Amazon.com's success." (S038) / "We want to fight entropy. The bar has to continuously go up." (S039)
    Sources: 4 (S004, S011, S038, S039). Confidence: high.

18. **High standards are teachable and domain specific; most misses come from underestimating the work.**
    "Unrealistic beliefs on scope - often hidden and undiscussed - kill high standards." (S065)
    Sources: 1 (S065). Confidence: low (one source, but a full letter on it).

19. **Pick hard operational problems on purpose, because difficulty is what creates advantage.**
    "We like things to be hard because then you can get competitive advantage from it." (S008) / "Taking professional pride in operational excellence. And so I'm talking about the details that nobody but you will ever know." (S024)
    Sources: 4 (S003, S008, S024, S025). Confidence: high.

20. **Be realistic about the odds and use early capital to remove risk, not to seek it.**
    "good entrepreneurs don't like Risk they seek to reduce risk" (S029) / "I would always tell people I thought there was a 70% chance they would lose their investment." (S001)
    Sources: 5 (S001, S008, S029, S043, S045). Confidence: high.

21. **Expand rather than defend, and lean into big trends instead of fighting them.**
    "we are gonna work like crazy to expand not to defend the territory we have now" (S004) / "If you fight them, you're probably fighting the future. Embrace them and you have a tailwind." (S064)
    Sources: 3 (S004, S030, S064). Confidence: high.

22. **Do not enter a market with a me-too offer; enter only when you have something different that delights.**
    "Our culture is much better at pioneering and inventing, so we have to have something that's different." (S044) / "we don't want to do a me-too product" (S037)
    Sources: 5 (S009, S037, S044, S054, S066). Confidence: high.

23. **A small startup should be as narrow and focused as possible.**
    "be as narrow and as focused as is possible to be" (S018) / "startup companies need to be absolutely laser focused" (S027)
    Sources: 3 (S018, S027, S046). Confidence: high.

24. **Give up on things, but not easily, and never throw good money after bad.**
    "You should give up on things but you shouldn't give up easily." (S010) / "the alternative--investing more of our own capital in these companies to keep them afloat--would have been an even bigger mistake." (S048)
    Sources: 3 (S010, S036, S048). Confidence: high.

25. **Missionaries beat mercenaries, in product quality and usually in money.**
    "missionaries build better products." (S055)
    Sources: 6 (S028, S029, S036, S037, S044, S055). Confidence: high.

26. **Senior leaders make a few high-quality decisions, on a few big ideas, years ahead of the current quarter.**
    "As a senior executive, you get paid to make a small number of high-quality decisions." (S044) / "Senior leaders don't have to do a lot of things. They have to pick the few big ideas and enforce tough execution against those big ideas." (S025)
    Sources: 5 (S001, S003, S014, S025, S044). Confidence: high.

27. **Create more than you consume; good business is not zero-sum.**
    "If you want to be successful in business (in life, actually), you have to create more than you consume." (S068) / "nobody else has to fail for us to do well" (S037)
    Sources: 5 (S013, S037, S041, S059, S068). Confidence: high.

28. **Founders need near-delusional optimism and should assume every problem is solvable.**
    "entrepreneurs need to be optimistic almost to the point of delusion." (S023) / "Being resourceful. If there's a problem, there's a solution." (S047)
    Sources: 5 (S001, S007, S008, S023, S047). Confidence: high.
