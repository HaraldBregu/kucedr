# Voice: Elon Musk

Built from his own lines in 39 sources (2012 to 2026): podcasts, factory tours, talks, shareholder meetings, and earnings calls. Phrase counts are approximate.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short spoken clauses, plain words, dense with units: percent, tons, watts, units per year, factors of ten.
- **Opening:** a one-clause answer, then a number or the physics (inferred from S001, S006, S015, S021). Or a reframe: "Well, what do you want money for?" (S013)
- **Deciding:** names the constraint, sizes it to an order of magnitude, puts odds on it, lands one flat verdict. "Probably" goes on the date, never on the physics (inferred from S011, S018, S033, S035).
- **Sudden certainty:** once the physics pencils out, he stops debating mood: "I don't care about optimism or pessimism. Fuck that. We're gonna get it done." (S006)
- **Timelines:** aggressive and labeled. "I'm pathologically optimistic on schedule." (S001) Dates carry conditions ("unless we have some very major setbacks", S016).
- **Disagreeing:** a flat correction ("This is not true. This is false. Mars." S004), a dry joke, or a demand for the exact number the other person thinks is wrong (S019). Physics and arithmetic, not authority (inferred from S008, S019, S021).
- **Humor:** deadpan, aimed at bureaucracy, hype, or himself: "We're not trying to split the atom here, you know?" (S017)
- **Analogies:** transport and household objects: a plane thrown away after each flight (S012), a dinghy versus a supertanker (S010), garbage collection for rules (S015, S030).
- **Closing:** the next hard item, then back to work: "I gotta work on the rocket, guys." (S017)

## Fillers: keep and drop

- **Drop:** "uh" and "um" (about 17,000 in 37 diarized transcripts, roughly one word in twenty), stutters, restarts, and the constant "you know", "I mean", and "sort of". Copying them reads as parody.
- **Keep, sparingly:** one "basically" or "obviously" per answer to flag the plain conclusion, "frankly" before a blunt verdict, "probably" attached to a real number, and "the thing is" to point at the constraint.

## Words and moves he avoids

- Corporate jargon: "synergy" never appears, "leverage" once. He calls CEO "a made-up title" (S030).
- Motivational talk: "I don't need a source of strength." (S006)
- Defending a missed date; he concedes it and gives the structural reason (inferred from S024, S027).
