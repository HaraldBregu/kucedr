# Principles: Elon Musk

29 decision rules, built from the 39 sources in `sources.md` (2012 to 2026), weighted toward execution speed and engineering decisions. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Where his view changed, both dates are given and the newer view is marked.

1. **Question every requirement, no matter who gave it to you.** "everyone's wrong some of the time." (S014) "start somewhere and then, you know, really be prepared to question your assumptions, uh, fix what you did wrong, and, uh, a-adapt to reality." (S023) Sources: 3 (S008, S014, S023). Confidence: high.

2. **Delete before you simplify, and keep deleting until some things have to come back.** "If you're not, uh, occasionally adding things back in, you are not deleting enough." (S014) "you can basically make in case arguments for so many things" (S014) Sources: 9 (S006, S009, S011, S012, S014, S017, S019, S034, S038). Confidence: high.

3. **Never optimize a thing that should not exist; when the premise is wrong, start over instead of modifying.** "possibly the most common error of a smart engineer is to optimize a thing that should not exist." (S014) "Just knock the house down and build a new one." (S008) Sources: 4 (S008, S011, S014, S023). Confidence: high.

4. **Do not speed up or automate a process you have not questioned, cut, and simplified.** "if you're digging, you know, your grave, don't dig it faster. Stop digging your grave." (S014) His own Model 3 mistake: "literally I automated, accelerated, simplified, and then deleted." (S014) Sources: 2 (S014, S024). Confidence: medium.

5. **Work on the limiting factor and leave what is working alone.** "Basically, if something is working well and making good progress, then there’s no point in me spending time on it." (S002) "and then we take actions to address the limiting factor." (S033) Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S013, S033, S035, S039). Confidence: high.

6. **A prototype is not progress; volume output is.** "it's not about press releases, it's about tonnage." (S024) "I've often said prototypes are, are easy, production is hard." (S006) Sources: 9 (S006, S008, S009, S014, S022, S024, S028, S032, S034). Confidence: high.

7. **When something is expensive, find out whether physics or process makes it so.** "that sets the actual floor on what a rocket, uh, can cost." (S021) "what's actually causing things to be expensive is how you put the atoms into the desired shape." (S006) Sources: 5 (S003, S006, S015, S021, S033). Confidence: high.

8. **Speed is the default; time is the one input you cannot buy back.** "the one thing you cannot replace is time." (S009) "I have a maniacal sense of urgency. So that maniacal sense of urgency projects through the rest of the company." (S002) Sources: 5 (S002, S009, S021, S029, S031). Confidence: high.

9. **Set the internal deadline you would hit about half the time, because schedules expand.** "it will expand to fill the available schedule and it’ll take five years" (S002) "I generally believe, um, in terms of internal, uh, timelines, that we wanna set, set the most aggressive timeline that we can." (S027) Sources: 2 (S002, S027). Confidence: medium.

10. **Stay optimistic inside the company, and hedge what you promise outside it.** "it’s better to err on the side of optimism and be wrong than err on the side of pessimism and be right, for quality of life." (S002) Newer view (2026): "that's not a timeframe we control." (S036) Changed over time: in 2019 he set hard public dates ("I'd be shocked if it's not next year at the latest" (S005)); from 2024 on he says not to set your watch by his dates (S015) and labels targets aspirational (S033, 2025). Sources: 7 (S001, S002, S005, S015, S027, S033, S036). Confidence: high.

11. **Get one version working first, then optimize it.** "The first order of business is to get one way at all that works." (S011) "first just gotta, like, make the damping work, and then we'll optimize it." (S014) Sources: 4 (S001, S002, S011, S014). Confidence: high.

12. **Fail fast where nobody gets hurt; be conservative where people do.** "The early ones, you want them to blow up, or you're gonna have to find a place to store them." (S009) "you can't just be blowing stuff up 'cause you're gonna kill people." (S009) Sources: 7 (S008, S009, S011, S012, S018, S034, S039). Confidence: high.

13. **Spend the test effort on what goes wrong, and break things on purpose before customers do.** "we ignore the ones that are where everything goes right" (S012) "we wanted to see where, where it would break, and, um, we, we found out where it would break." (S037) Sources: 4 (S011, S012, S014, S037). Confidence: high.

14. **Physics and reality are the judge, not rank or reputation.** "physics is the law, everything else is a recommendation." (S001) "Physics is a harsh judge." (S018) Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S006, S018, S021, S028). Confidence: high.

15. **Put a number on every claim and every risk.** "which one of those numbers do you think we have wrong, and what numbers do you think are correct?" (S019) "I think you have to just think of these things as probabilities, not certainties." (S020) Sources: 7 (S006, S010, S011, S018, S019, S020, S033). Confidence: high.

16. **Ask for negative feedback, and let outsiders grade the work.** "people just view, like, positive feedback like water off a duck's back." (S003) "instead of kind of grading your own homework, you would at least have competitors grading your homework." (S018) Sources: 4 (S003, S018, S021, S032). Confidence: high.

17. **If a supplier is slower than you need, build it yourself, and do not inherit their limits.** "to the degree that you inherit the legacy supply chain, you inhegi-inherit the legacy constraints, including their speed, uh, cost, and, uh, and technology." (S017) "quite frankly, it'd be crazy not to try the tera-terafab." (S039) He integrates from need, not preference: "we build them out of desperation" (S039). Sources: 7 (S002, S016, S017, S027, S033, S035, S039). Confidence: high.

18. **Every requirement and every recurring cost needs a person who owns it.** "you can't ask the department. You have to ask a person." (S014) "and no one ever turned the money off." (S012) Sources: 5 (S012, S013, S014, S019, S020). Confidence: high.

19. **The person in charge must know how the product works and spend time where it is made.** "we don't have a ivory tower management or engineering, and that the management engineering is, uh, as close to the factory as possible." (S030) "I don't actually have an office here. I, I either walk the floor or I hold meetings in this room." (S029) Sources: 7 (S006, S014, S015, S021, S024, S029, S030). Confidence: high.

20. **Hire for evidence of exceptional ability, and trust the conversation over the resume.** "don’t look at the resume. Just believe your interaction." (S002) "People are people. There’s no magical pixie dust." (S002) Sources: 4 (S002, S017, S020, S032). Confidence: high.

21. **If you start it and care about it, run it yourself.** "you gotta play the hand yourself." (S003) "it's either that or the company's gonna die." (S017) He calls not starting Tesla with JB Straubel alone "the worst business decision I ever made" (S024). Sources: 3 (S003, S017, S024). Confidence: high.

22. **Keep cash for irrational times, and keep enough control to protect a long horizon.** "watch your cash flow, um, and get to c-positive cash flow as soon as you can." (S017) "It's not a money thing; it's a, a reasonable control thing over the future of the company" (S026) Sources: 6 (S008, S013, S017, S019, S023, S026). Confidence: high.

23. **Do hard things; a small chance at something important beats no chance.** "I don't know how you create value by solving easy problems." (S039) "a small chance of success is better than no chance of success." (S021) Sources: 4 (S021, S022, S028, S039). Confidence: high.

24. **Accept the odds up front, and take drastic action only when success is no longer possible.** "if you just, if you just accept the probabilities, um, then that diminishes fear." (S022) "I’ll take drastic action only when I conclude that success is not in a set of possible outcomes." (S002) Sources: 4 (S002, S015, S021, S022). Confidence: high.

25. **Build useful things and let the money follow.** "If you do that, then money will come as a natural consequence of that, uh, as opposed to pursuing money directly." (S020) "I really was just trying to be useful. That's the optimization." (S022) Sources: 5 (S006, S020, S021, S022, S029). Confidence: high.

26. **Hard problems need serious hours in person, but your hours are not everyone's quota.** "you definitely need to put in serious hours." (S020) "Like, I work seven days a week, but, but I, I'm not expecting others to do that." (S029) In 2023 he also called remote work "morally wrong" (S029), a stance the other sources do not repeat. Sources: 4 (S008, S020, S027, S029). Confidence: high.

27. **Protect focus: one main quest, few side quests, little context switching.** "I wouldn't recommend running two companies." (S023) "I gotta get back to the main quest here." (S021) Sources: 7 (S007, S008, S012, S016, S021, S023, S031). Confidence: high.

28. **When a category is flipping, build the new thing, not variants of the old one, and retire the old line.** "They should have made the iPhone." (S034) "If they had just gone EV2, EV3, man, they would've just owned the world." (S008) In 2026 he ended Model S and X to free factory space for Optimus (S039). Sources: 4 (S005, S008, S034, S039). Confidence: high.

29. **When a shift is coming anyway, be a participant, not a spectator.** "really there's two choices here, either be a spectator or a part- or a participant." (S012) Changed over time: in 2019 he wanted a government agency over AI before anything else (S004), and he argued for slowing AI for years; newer (2025 to 2026) he joins in to steer it (S012, S013, S035). Sources: 5 (S004, S012, S013, S021, S035). Confidence: high.
